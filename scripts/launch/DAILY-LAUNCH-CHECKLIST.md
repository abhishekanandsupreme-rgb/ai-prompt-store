## ONGOING: THE SUSTAIN PHASE (Days 16-30+)
**Theme: Systems Over Hustle**

### Weekly Rhythm (Days 16-30)
- **Monday**: Review last week's metrics, plan this week's content
- **Tuesday**: Content creation day (2 blog posts, 5 social posts, 1 video)
- **Wednesday**: Outreach day (20 DMs, 5 emails, 5 partnership follow-ups)
- **Thursday**: Community engagement day (host event, answer questions)
- **Friday**: Ad optimization day (review, kill losers, scale winners)
- **Weekend**: Rest + strategic planning for next week

### Daily Minimums (Days 16-30)
- [ ] Post at least once on 2 social platforms
- [ ] Answer all customer messages within 12 hours
- [ ] Create 1 piece of long-form content (blog / video) per week
- [ ] Reach out to 5 potential partners per week
- [ ] Review analytics daily, make one optimization

## EMERGENCY CHECKLIST

### If Traffic Drops >50% From Baseline
- [ ] Check for platform algorithm changes
- [ ] Verify paid ads are still running
- [ ] Check product page for errors
- [ ] Post emergency content with new angle
- [ ] Engage directly with audience to diagnose issue

### If Sales Stall for 3+ Days
- [ ] Review competitor activity (are they launching?)
- [ ] Refresh product page copy and images
- [ ] Run limited-time promotion or bonus
- [ ] Send re-engagement email to list
- [ ] Test new traffic source

### If Negative Feedback Increases
- [ ] Address publicly and transparently
- [ ] Offer refunds or fixes immediately
- [ ] Update product if feedback is valid
- [ ] Communicate improvements to audience
