# FINAL-LAUNCH-SEQUENCE.md
## 48-Hour Minute-by-Minute Launch Playbook
### From First Live Product to First Sale

---

## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH PREPARATION (Hours -24 to 0)
**Goal: Everything is ready before the clock starts**

### Hour -24: Final Asset Verification
- [ ] Product files (PDFs, templates, scripts) are finalized and virus-scanned
- [ ] Cover images generated and uploaded to Gumroad
- [ ] Product description copy proofread (headline, bullets, CTA)
- [ ] Price set with one click-preview link
- [ ] All social accounts logged in and two-factor ready

### Hour -12: Infrastructure Check
- [ ] Email list loaded into autoresponder (if any)
- [ ] Thank-you page URL confirmed
- [ ] Google Analytics / tracking pixels verified
- [ ] Backup payment method active on Gumroad account

### Hour -2: Team Briefing (Solo or Team)
- [ ] Post-launch roles assigned (responder, tracker, community manager)
- [ ] Slack/Discord/Group chat created for launch coordination
- [ ] Shared launch tracker (Google Sheet / Notion) opened

### Hour -1: The "Nothing Left to Do" Moment
- [ ] Close all non-essential tabs
- [ ] Clear browser cache (or use incognito)
- [ ] Phone on Do Not Disturb except for launch team alerts

---

## PHASE 2: LAUNCH DAY (Hours 0 to 24)
**Goal: Maximum visibility and initial conversion momentum**

### HOUR 0:00 — GO LIVE
- [ ] Click "Publish" on Gumroad
- [ ] Confirm product page loads on desktop AND mobile
- [ ] Copy live product URL
- [ ] Post to own social channels (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok)
- [ ] Send launch email to list (if any)

### HOUR 0:15 — Immediate Distribution Blitz
- [ ] Post in 3-5 relevant Reddit communities (read rules first)
- [ ] Share in 2-3 Facebook groups where prompt engineering is discussed
- [ ] Post in Discord servers where AI creators hang out
- [ ] Pin launch tweet/thread to profile

### HOUR 0:30 — Influencer & Outreach Ping
- [ ] DM 5-10 micro-influencers (500–10k followers) in AI/creator space
- [ ] Email 5-10 newsletter owners with short, non-spammy pitch
- [ ] Submit product to relevant directories (AI tool directories, prompt libraries)

### HOUR 1:00 — First Sale Check
- [ ] Refresh Gumroad dashboard
- [ ] If sale: Post "First sale!" to personal channels (social proof)
- [ ] If no sale: Check product page load speed, copy clarity, pricing display

### HOUR 2:00 — Community Engagement
- [ ] Respond to every comment on launch posts
- [ ] Answer DMs within 5 minutes
- [ ] Engage with 10+ posts from target audience (be helpful, not pitchy)

### HOUR 4:00 — Content Seeding
- [ ] Publish blog post / Medium article about the problem solved
- [ ] Post 1-2 demo clips on TikTok / Reels / Shorts showing the product in action
- [ ] Create 3 Pinterest pins with product value proposition

### HOUR 6:00 — Paid Amplification (Optional, Budget Permitting)
- [ ] Run $20-50 Twitter/X promoted posts targeting AI prompt enthusiasts
- [ ] Run $20-50 Reddit promoted posts in relevant subreddits (if allowed)
- [ ] Run $20-50 Facebook/Instagram ads targeting "prompt engineering" interests

### HOUR 8:00 — Mid-Flight Optimization
- [ ] Review analytics: Which traffic source is converting best?
- [ ] Double down on top performer (post more in that community / boost that ad)
- [ ] Pause underperforming ads immediately
- [ ] Update product description based on FAQ in comments

### HOUR 12:00 — Halfway Through Day 1
- [ ] Review total sales and revenue
- [ ] Send second social blast (different angle, same product)
- [ ] Share customer testimonial if any (even early DM praise)
- [ ] Post behind-the-scenes content about building the prompt pack

### HOUR 16:00 — Evening Push
- [ ] Post in evening-active communities (Discord, Twitter evening hours)
- [ ] Share on Hacker News "Show HN" if applicable (follow guidelines strictly)
- [ ] Engage with trending AI/prompt tweets to ride algorithmic waves

### HOUR 20:00 — Late-Night Organic
- [ ] Respond to any overseas traffic (Asia/Europe time zones)
- [ ] Update FAQ section on product page
- [ ] Draft tomorrow's launch sequence content

### HOUR 24:00 — Day 1 Close-Out
- [ ] Export Gumroad sales report
- [ ] Log metrics: visitors, conversion rate, revenue, traffic sources
- [ ] Plan Day 2 priorities based on Day 1 learnings
- [ ] Rest. Do not burn out before the 48-hour window closes.

---

## PHASE 3: DAY 2 (Hours 24 to 48)
**Goal: Sustain momentum, optimize, and convert early interest**

### HOUR 24:30 — Early Bird Momentum
- [ ] Post "Day 1 results" or "almost sold out" if inventory is limited (creates FOMO)
- [ ] Share highlight reel of Day 1 wins (sales numbers, community response)

### HOUR 26:00 — Email Follow-Up Sequence (if list exists)
- [ ] Send reminder email with different angle / use case
- [ ] Include social proof quote from Day 1 buyer
- [ ] Link directly to purchase page with clear CTA

### HOUR 28:00 — Content Repurposing
- [ ] Turn Day 1 top-performing tweet into a thread
- [ ] Turn demo clips into carousels / slide decks
- [ ] Write follow-up blog post with advanced use case

### HOUR 30:00 — Partnership Activation
- [ ] Follow up with influencers/newsletters who didn't respond on Day 1
- [ ] Offer affiliate commission (Gumroad has built-in affiliate system)
- [ ] Provide them with ready-made copy, images, and demo videos

### HOUR 32:00 — Mid-Day 2 Optimization
- [ ] A/B test headline on product page (if Gumroad allows minor edits without republishing)
- [ ] Check cart abandonment (Gumroad doesn't track this natively, but watch for "viewed but no sale")
- [ ] Consider limited-time bonus for Day 2 buyers only

### HOUR 34:00 — Community Deep-Dive
- [ ] Host 15-minute Twitter Spaces / Clubhouse / Discord voice chat about prompt engineering
- [ ] Drop product link in chat at natural value moment (not spam-first)
- [ ] Answer questions in real-time

### HOUR 36:00 — Press & Media Outreach
- [ ] Pitch to AI-focused blogs and newsletters
- [ ] Post on ProductHunt (if eligible and product fits)
- [ ] Submit to AI tool aggregator sites

### HOUR 38:00 — Paid Retargeting (If Traffic is High)
- [ ] Set up retargeting audience of people who visited product page but didn't buy
- [ ] Run 20% off / bonus incentive ad for retargeting audience only
- [ ] Increase daily ad budget by 25% if ROAS > 2x

### HOUR 40:00 — Customer Success & Scaling
- [ ] Send personalized thank-you DMs to early buyers
- [ ] Ask for testimonials / reviews in exchange for bonus tip sheet
- [ ] Document buyer questions to create FAQ / knowledge base

### HOUR 42:00 — Viral Content Push
- [ ] Post controversial or counterintuitive take about prompts (engagement bait)
- [ ] Tag relevant AI accounts that might retweet / engage
- [ ] Run small giveaway (free copy in exchange for retweet + tag)

### HOUR 44:00 — Partnership Deep-Dive
- [ ] Provide affiliate partners with updated sales numbers to motivate them
- [ ] Create co-branded content with top-performing promoter
- [ ] Bundle deals with complementary products

### HOUR 46:00 — Last 2-Hour Blitz
- [ ] "Last chance for early pricing" messaging (if changing price after launch)
- [ ] Post final reminder across all channels
- [ ] Engage with anyone who commented in last 48 hours but hasn't bought

### HOUR 48:00 — 48-Hour Close-Out & Pivot
- [ ] Final sales tally
- [ ] Calculate total ROI (time + ad spend vs. revenue)
- [ ] Identify best traffic source and double down for next 7 days
- [ ] Pause / kill all paid ads if ROAS < 1.5x
- [ ] Transition from "launch mode" to "sustain mode" for Days 3-7
- [ ] Archive launch data for future product launches

## EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### If Product Page Breaks
1. Gumroad status page / Twitter for platform outages
2. Have backup link ready (different Gumroad product, temporary Stripe/PayPal link)
3. Communicate transparently to audience: "Having technical issues, fixing now"

### If Spam / Negative Comments
1. Do not engage publicly with trolls
2. Hide/delete if violating platform rules
3. Address genuine concerns politely and publicly to show accountability

### If Sales Are Zero After 12 Hours
1. Check product page mobile experience (most traffic is mobile)
2. Verify Gumroad payment methods are working (test buy with real card if needed)
3. Audit copy: Is the value proposition clear in 5 seconds?
4. Check competitor pricing (are we priced out of market?)
5. Switch outreach to personal DMs instead of public posts

### If Sales Are Explosive (>100 in first 24h)
1. Monitor Gumroad for fraud / chargebacks
2. Prepare fulfillment batch (send files, welcome email)
3. Have customer support templates ready for common questions
4. Do NOT increase price mid-launch — save price increase for post-launch period
