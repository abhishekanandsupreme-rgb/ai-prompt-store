**Model:** Single-tier / Mid-ticket

### Our Competitive Advantage
- Professional cover design (not generic text)
- Free sample prompts (lower risk for buyer)
- Tiered pricing ($7 starter / $29 pro / $97 ultimate)
- Actual testimonials and ratings
- Post-purchase email sequence with bonus content

## WHAT WE SHOULD IMPROVE (DIFFERENTIATORS)

### 1. Visual Branding
**Problem:** Almost all competitors use generic Gumroad layouts with basic text covers.
**Solution:** Invest in professional cover design. Use brand colors, typography, and visual hierarchy.
**Impact:** +20-30% click-through rate from Gumroad Discover and social shares.

### 2. Social Proof System
**Problem:** Competitors have 0-5 reviews. Most have none.
**Solution:** Seed initial reviews from beta testers. Add video testimonials. Show usage metrics ("Used by 1,200+ creators").
**Impact:** +40-60% conversion rate improvement.

### 3. Onboarding & Success Path
**Problem:** Competitors dump 100 prompts in a PDF and say "good luck."
**Solution:** Create "Prompt #1 to get your first result in 10 minutes" guide. Add video walkthroughs.
**Impact:** +30% fewer refunds, +50% more word-of-mouth.

### 4. Guarantee
**Problem:** Almost none offer a meaningful guarantee.
**Solution:** 30-day "Keep the Product" guarantee. "Use the prompts for 30 days. If you don't save at least 5 hours, keep the prompts and get a full refund."
**Impact:** +15-25% conversion rate improvement.

### 5. Community Access
**Problem:** No competitor offers buyer community.
**Solution:** Include access to private Discord/Telegram for buyers.
**Impact:** Higher perceived value, repeat purchases, organic word-of-mouth.

### 6. Email Nurture Sequence
**Problem:** Competitors send purchase confirmation and nothing else.
**Solution:** 7-email sequence over 30 days: quick wins, case studies, advanced tips, upsells.
**Impact:** +20-40% increase in customer lifetime value.

### 7. Dynamic Updates
**Problem:** Competitors sell static packs that become outdated.
**Solution:** Monthly "Prompt Drop" emails with new prompts for new AI features.
**Impact:** Turns one-time buyers into recurring subscribers.
