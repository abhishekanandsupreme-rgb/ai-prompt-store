# First Sale Playbook — What to Do in the First 24 Hours After Your First Sale

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-02

Your first sale is the most important moment in your Gumroad journey. This playbook tells you exactly what to do — minute by minute — to maximize momentum, build social proof, and set up repeat sales.

---

## The First 5 Minutes: Celebrate & Capture

### 0:00 — SALE NOTIFICATION RECEIVED
- [ ] **Screenshot the sale notification** (Gumroad email or dashboard)
- [ ] **Do NOT announce yet** — wait for the buyer to receive their download
- [ ] **Check the buyer's name** (if visible) for personalization

### 0:02 — VERIFY THE SALE
- [ ] Open Gumroad dashboard → Sales tab
- [ ] Confirm payment status is "Complete" (not "Pending")
- [ ] Note the product purchased
- [ ] Note the amount paid

### 0:05 — FIRST SOCIAL PROOF POST
Post this to your personal social accounts (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram Stories):

> "Just made my first sale on Gumroad! 🎉
> 
> [Product name] is officially live and someone just bought it.
> 
> If you've been on the fence, now's the time to grab it → [product URL]
> 
> #indiehacker #gumroad #buildinpublic"

---

## First 30 Minutes: Amplify & Engage

### 0:10 — THANK THE BUYER (if possible)
- [ ] If buyer's email is visible, send a brief thank-you:
  > "Hey [Name]! Just wanted to say thanks for being my first customer. Hope you love the prompts! If you have any questions, just reply to this email."
- [ ] Do NOT ask for a review yet — let them use the product first

### 0:15 — POST IN COMMUNITIES
Share in 2-3 relevant communities (Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups):

> "I just published my first product on Gumroad — [product name]. It's a pack of 50 AI prompts for [use case]. Just got my first sale! If you're interested in [topic], check it out: [URL]"

**Where to post:**
- r/ChatGPT
- r/promptengineering
- r/SideProject
- r/indiehackers
- AI-related Discord servers
- Facebook groups for AI creators

### 0:20 — UPDATE YOUR SOCIAL BIOS
- [ ] Add Gumroad link to Twitter/X bio
- [ ] Add Gumroad link to LinkedIn featured section
- [ ] Add "Now selling on Gumroad" to Instagram bio
- [ ] Pin a tweet about the product

### 0:30 — FIRST METRICS SNAPSHOT
Record these numbers in a tracking sheet:
- [ ] Time of first sale: ___________
- [ ] Product purchased: ___________
- [ ] Revenue: ___________
- [ ] Traffic sources so far: ___________
- [ ] Conversion rate (if known): ___________

## First 2 Hours: Content Blitz

### 1:00 — CREATE "FIRST SALE" CONTENT
- [ ] Write a Twitter/X thread about the journey:
  1. "I just made my first Gumroad sale. Here's what I learned..."
  2. "I built [product] because [problem]"
  3. "It took me [X hours/days] to create"
  4. "I almost gave up because [obstacle]"
  5. "But today, someone paid me for it"
  6. "If you're thinking about selling digital products, start now"
  7. "Here's the link: [URL]"

### 1:30 — POST DEMO CONTENT
- [ ] Record a 30-second screen recording showing the product
- [ ] Post to TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts
- [ ] Caption: "50 AI prompts that will save you hours. Link in bio."

### 2:00 — ENGAGE WITH EVERYONE
- [ ] Reply to every comment on your launch posts
- [ ] Like and retweet/share any mentions
- [ ] DM 5 people who engaged with your content (thank them, no pitch)

## First 6 Hours: Outreach Acceleration

### 3:00 — INFLUENCER OUTREACH
DM 5-10 micro-influencers in your niche:

> "Hey [Name]! I just launched a pack of 50 AI prompts for [use case]. I'm a fan of your content on [specific topic]. Would love to send you a free copy if you're interested. No strings attached — just think your audience might find it useful."

### 4:00 — EMAIL OUTREACH
Email 5-10 newsletter owners or bloggers:

> "Subject: Quick question about [their publication]
> 
> Hi [Name],
> 
> I've been following [publication] for a while — love your piece on [specific article].
> 
> I just launched a product that might interest your readers: [product name] — 50 AI prompts for [use case].
> 
> Would you consider mentioning it? Happy to provide an exclusive discount for your audience.
> 
> Either way, keep up the great work!
> 
> [Your name]"

### 5:00 — CROSS-PROMOTION OUTREACH
- [ ] Find 3-5 complementary product owners (not competitors)
- [ ] Propose cross-promotion: "I'll mention your product if you mention mine"
- [ ] Offer bundle deals or exclusive discounts

### 6:00 — MID-DAY METRICS CHECK
- [ ] Total sales today: ___________
- [ ] Total revenue today: ___________
- [ ] Traffic sources ranked: ___________
- [ ] Best-performing content: ___________

- [ ] Offer bonus content to early buyers

## First 24 Hours: Lock In Gains

### 16:00 — EVENING PUSH
- [ ] Post in evening-active communities
- [ ] Engage with trending AI/prompt tweets
- [ ] Share behind-the-scenes content about building the product

### 20:00 — OVERSEAS ENGAGEMENT
- [ ] Respond to comments from different time zones
- [ ] Post in UK/EU-active communities (evening in US = morning in EU)
- [ ] Engage with Asia-Pacific morning traffic

### 24:00 — DAY 1 CLOSE-OUT
- [ ] Export Gumroad sales report
- [ ] Log all metrics in tracker
- [ ] Send thank-you to all buyers (if you have their emails)
- [ ] Plan Day 2 content based on what worked
- [ ] Rest. You earned it.

## What to Do If You Get Multiple Sales

### 2-5 Sales
- [ ] Post updated social proof: "X people bought today!"
- [ ] Ask first buyer for testimonial
- [ ] Create urgency: "Launch pricing ends soon"

### 5-10 Sales
- [ ] Post a "thank you" to all buyers
- [ ] Share a specific use case from a buyer (with permission)
- [ ] Consider raising price slightly (test $12.99)

### 10+ Sales
- [ ] Post a detailed "journey" thread
- [ ] Reach out to media/blogs with social proof
- [ ] Create a limited-time bonus for next 10 buyers
- [ ] Start planning your next product

---

## What to Do If You Get NO Sales in 24 Hours

Don't panic. This is normal for day 1. Here's your recovery plan:

### Immediate Actions
- [ ] Check product page loads correctly (test on mobile)
- [ ] Verify "Buy" button works
- [ ] Confirm price displays correctly
- [ ] Check that download works after purchase

### Content Fixes
- [ ] Rewrite product headline (make it benefit-driven)
- [ ] Add more specific use cases to description
- [ ] Create a video demo showing the product
- [ ] Post in 5 more communities

### Outreach Fixes
- [ ] DM 10 more influencers
- [ ] Email 10 more newsletter owners
- [ ] Post in 3 more subreddits
- [ ] Ask a friend to share

### Pricing Test
- [ ] Try a 24-hour flash sale ($4.99 for next 24 hours)
- [ ] Offer "pay what you want" with $5 minimum
- [ ] Bundle 2 products at a discount

## Key Principles

1. **Speed matters** — Post about your first sale within 5 minutes
2. **Social proof compounds** — Each sale makes the next one easier
3. **Engage genuinely** — Reply to every comment, DM, and email
4. **Track everything** — Data beats intuition
5. **Don't stop** — One sale means your product works. Keep pushing.
