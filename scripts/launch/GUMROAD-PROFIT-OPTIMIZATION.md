## 1. PRICING EXPERIMENTS

### Pricing Psychology Framework

| Strategy | Application | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Charm Pricing** | End prices in 7, 9, or 5 | $27, $49, $97 |
| **Price Anchoring** | Show higher price first, then "sale" price | ~~$197~~ $47 |
| **Decoy Effect** | Add middle option that makes target look better | Basic $19 / Pro $49 / Ultimate $47 (decoy) |
| **Tiered Value** | 3 clearly differentiated tiers | Good / Better / Best |
| **Urgency Pricing** | Limited-time launch discount | $97 → $47 (48 hours only) |

### Pricing Experiment Matrix

Run these experiments sequentially. Test ONE variable at a time.

#### Experiment 1: Launch Price Discovery
**Hypothesis:** You don't know your true price until you test.
**Method:**
1. Set initial price at 50% of your target long-term price
2. Run for 7 days
3. If conversion rate > 5%, price is likely too low
4. If conversion rate < 1%, price is likely too high
5. Adjust in $5-10 increments until conversion stabilizes at 2-4%

**Target Price Ranges by Product Type:**
- Quick-reference cheat sheet: $7-$19
- Template pack (10-20 files): $19-$47
- Comprehensive course/workbook: $47-$197
- Done-for-you service: $197-$997

#### Experiment 2: Price Anchoring Test
**Hypothesis:** Showing a higher MSRP increases perceived value.
**Method:**
1. Add "~~$199~~" strikethrough to your $47 price
2. Create separate product page with clean $47 (no strikethrough)
3. Split traffic 50/50
4. Measure conversion rate difference

**Expected Outcome:** 10-25% conversion lift with strikethrough

#### Experiment 3: Name-Your-Price
**Hypothesis:** Removing price friction increases conversion.
**Method:**
1. Create "Pay What You Want" version
2. Set minimum at 50% of standard price
3. Set suggested price at standard price
4. Track average payment vs. conversion rate

**Warning:** Only run if you have strong social proof and audience trust.

#### Experiment 4: Bundle Price Elasticity
**Hypothesis:** Bundles increase AOV more than they reduce conversion.
**Method:**
1. Create 2-product bundle at 70% of combined individual prices
2. Track bundle vs. individual purchases
3. Calculate: Bundle Revenue = (Bundle Price × Bundle Sales) + (Individual Price × Individual Sales)
4. Compare to pre-bundle revenue

#### Experiment 5: Subscription vs. One-Time
**Hypothesis:** Recurring revenue outweighs lower initial commitment.
**Method:**
1. Offer one-time $197 OR subscription $19/month
2. Track: conversion rate, 90-day retention, LTV
3. If subscription 90-day retention < 40%, one-time may be better

## 2. BUNDLE STRATEGIES

### Bundle Architecture

```
ENTRY POINT (Low Price, High Volume)
    └── Single Prompt Pack — $19
         │
         ├── UPSELL PATH 1: Upgrade to Pro
         │    └── Pro Pack — $47 (includes Entry + 2x more prompts + video tutorials)
         │         │
         │         └── UPSELL PATH 2: Ultimate Bundle
         │              └── Ultimate — $97 (Pro + exclusive community + monthly updates)
         │
         └── UPSELL PATH 2: Complementary Bundle
              └── Prompt Pack + Template Pack — $39 (save $10)
```

### Bundle Types to Create

#### 1. The "Complete Collection" Bundle
- **What:** All products in your store at 30-40% off
- **When:** After you have 3+ products
- **Pricing:** Sum of individual prices × 0.6
- **Psychology:** "Everything you'll ever need"

#### 2. The "Niche Stack" Bundle
- **What:** Products that solve a specific job-to-be-done
- **Example:** "Social Media Manager Starter Pack" (LinkedIn prompts + caption templates + scheduling guide)
- **Pricing:** Sum × 0.7
- **Psychology:** "Built for [specific role]"

#### 3. The "Build-Your-Own" Bundle
- **What:** Customer selects 3+ products, gets 25% off
- **Implementation:** Gumroad doesn't support natively — use Zipify, Lemon Squeezy, or manual coupon
- **Psychology:** "Customize your toolkit"

#### 4. The "Insider" Bundle
- **What:** Product + private community + monthly updates
- **Pricing:** 2x product price
- **Psychology:** "Join the inner circle"

#### 5. The "Flash Bundle" (Time-Limited)
- **What:** Holiday / launch-specific bundle with physical or digital bonus
- **Example:** "Black Friday: Prompt Pack + Printable Planner + Audio Training"
- **Pricing:** Sum × 0.5 for 48 hours only
- **Psychology:** "Never available again"

### Bundle Pricing Math

| Bundle Name | Individual Sum | Bundle Price | Discount | Psychology Anchor |
|-------------|---------------|--------------|----------|-------------------|
| Quick Start | $19 + $29 = $48 | $29 | 40% off | "Get both for price of one" |
| Pro Suite | $47 + $47 + $29 = $123 | $79 | 36% off | "Complete toolkit" |
| Ultimate | $197 + $97 + $47 = $341 | $149 | 56% off | "Everything included" |
| Insider | $97 + $97/mo = $194/mo | $97/mo | 50% off | "Community + product" |

## 3. UPSELL PATHS

### The Upsell Funnel

```
AWARENESS → Free content (tweets, threads, blog posts)
     ↓
INTEREST → Lead magnet (free prompt pack, checklist)
     ↓
CONSIDERATION → Low-price entry product ($7-$19)
     ↓
PURCHASE → Thank-you page UPSELL
     ↓
POST-PURCHASE → Email sequence UPSELL
     ↓
LOYALTY → Community + recurring product
```

### Upsell 1: Thank-You Page Upsell (Highest Converting)
**Placement:** Immediately after purchase on Gumroad thank-you page
**Offer:** "Wait! Add [Product B] for just $X more (normally $Y)"
**Discount:** 30-50% off when added to current purchase
**Limit:** "This offer expires when you leave this page"

**Thank-You Page Upsell Templates:**

**Template A: The Upgrade**
> "Thanks for your purchase! Before you go... 70% of buyers like you also grab the [Pro Pack] which includes [specific benefit]. Add it now for just $27 extra (normally $67)."

**Template B: The Complementary**
> "Great choice! Customers who bought this also love [related product] to [solve related problem]. Get both for $X total — save $Y."

**Template C: The Insider**
> "You're in! Want exclusive access to our private community where we share new prompts every week? Add community access for $9/month."

### Upsell 2: Post-Purchase Email Sequence
**Timing:** Days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 after purchase

**Email 1 (Day 1): The Quick Win**
- Subject: "Your first result should be visible in 24 hours"
- Content: Quick tip to get value fast
- Upsell: "If you want 10x faster results, check out [Advanced Pack]"

**Email 3 (Day 3): The Case Study**
- Subject: "How Sarah went from zero to 10k followers in 14 days"
- Content: Case study using your product
- Upsell: "Sarah used [Pro Pack] — get the same templates"

**Email 7 (Day 7): The Expansion**
- Subject: "You've mastered X. Ready for Y?"
- Content: What they've learned, what's next
- Upsell: "[Next Level Product] is now open"

**Email 14 (Day 14): The Renewal**
- Subject: "Two weeks in. Here's what's next."
- Content: Advanced technique, community invite
- Upsell: "Join the Insider program — 200+ prompts + weekly updates"

**Email 30 (Day 30): The Loyalty**
- Subject: "One month later. How's it going?"
- Content: Survey, testimonial request
- Upsell: "Annual bundle — get everything for one low price"

### Upsell 3: In-Product Upsells
**Placement:** Inside PDFs, videos, and templates
**Technique:** Natural recommendation, not hard sell

**Example Copy:**
> "This template works great with the [LinkedIn Caption Pack] (page 12). If you haven't grabbed it yet, use code BUNDLE20 for 20% off."

### Upsell 4: Order Bump (Gumroad Native)
**Placement:** Checkout page before payment
**Offer:** Small, irresistible add-on at 70%+ off
**Examples:**
- "Add the 50 Bonus Prompts for just $7 extra"
- "Add the 'Prompt Engineering Masterclass' video for $17"
- "Add priority support for $9"

**Order Bump Best Practices:**
- Price must be < 20% of main product price
- Must be immediately consumable (no waiting)
- Clear, single-sentence benefit
- No choice paralysis (one offer only)

## 4. EMAIL FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCES

### Sequence A: Pre-Launch / Waitlist (5 Emails)

**Email 1 (Sign-up):**
- Subject: "You're on the list. Here's what's coming."
- Content: What the product is, when it launches, exclusive discount for waitlist
- CTA: "I'll notify you first"

**Email 2 (3 Days Before):**
- Subject: "3 days until [Product] goes live"
- Content: Sneak peek, problem agitation, early-bird pricing
- CTA: "Get early access"

**Email 3 (1 Day Before):**
- Subject: "Tomorrow. 9 AM EST. Set your alarm."
- Content: Final tease, social proof from beta testers, price reveal
- CTA: "Mark your calendar"

**Email 4 (Launch Day Morning):**
- Subject: "It's live. Here's your exclusive link."
- Content: Direct purchase link, early-bird bonus expires at midnight
- CTA: "Claim your copy"

**Email 5 (Launch Day Evening):**
- Subject: "Last chance for early-bird pricing"
- Content: Urgency, social proof ("32 sold in 6 hours"), price going up tomorrow
- CTA: "Buy now before price increases"

### Sequence C: Abandoned Cart (3 Emails)

**Email 1 (1 Hour After Abandonment):**
- Subject: "You left something behind..."

- CTA: "Complete your purchase"

## 5. REVENUE OPTIMIZATION TACTICS

### The 5 Levers of Gumroad Profit

1. **Traffic Volume** — More visitors = more sales
   - Tactics: SEO, social content, outreach, ads

2. **Conversion Rate** — Better page = more sales
   - Tactics: Copy testing, social proof, mobile optimization, page speed

3. **Average Order Value (AOV)** — Bigger carts = more revenue
   - Tactics: Bundles, upsells, order bumps, tiered pricing

4. **Purchase Frequency** — More buys over time
   - Tactics: Email sequences, new products, community, subscription

5. **Refund Rate** — Keep more money
   - Tactics: Better product previews, clearer expectations, quick support

### Quick Wins to Implement This Week

| Tactic | Time to Implement | Expected Revenue Impact |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|
| Add strikethrough pricing | 5 min | +10-15% conversion |
| Add order bump | 15 min | +20-30% AOV |
| Send abandoned cart emails | 30 min | +10-15% recovered sales |
| Add 3 testimonials | 20 min | +20% conversion |
| Mobile-optimize product images | 1 hour | +10% mobile conversion |
| Add FAQ section | 30 min | -20% support tickets |
| Create bundle offer | 2 hours | +15-25% AOV |

### Revenue Formula

```
Revenue = (Traffic × Conversion Rate) × Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency

Example:
(10,000 visitors × 3% conversion) × $47 AOV × 1.2 purchases/buyer
= 300 sales × $47 × 1.2
= $16,920
```

**To double revenue, improve any TWO of the three factors:**
- Double traffic: 20,000 visitors
- Double conversion: 6% (via better copy/offer)
- Double AOV: $94 (via bundles/upsells)
- Double frequency: 2.4 purchases/buyer (via more products/subscriptions)
