# AI Prompt Store - 15 Day Launch Guide

## Day 1: Setup & Platform (Today)
1. Create Gumroad account at gumroad.com (free)
2. Create 10 products with prices ($9.99 each, $27.99 bundle)
3. Upload prompt files as downloadable products
4. Set up payment processing (Stripe/PayPal automatic with Gumroad)

## Day 2-3: Website & Marketing Assets
1. Deploy landing page (Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages)
2. Create social media accounts:
   - Twitter/X: @AIPromptStore
   - Instagram: @ai.prompt.store
   - LinkedIn: AI Prompt Store
3. Design promotional graphics (Canva - free tier)

## Day 4-5: Content Creation
1. Write 10 blog posts about AI prompts/use cases
2. Create 3 YouTube videos showing prompt examples
3. Record Loom demo of product usage
4. Write Twitter thread about productivity with AI

## Day 6-7: Community Building
1. Post in 20+ Reddit communities (r/artificial, r/ChatGPT, r/productivity)
2. Share in 10+ Facebook groups
3. Post on Product Hunt (wait for optimal time)
4. Engage with AI/creator communities on Discord

## Day 8-10: Influencer Outreach
1. Identify 20 micro-influencers in AI/creator space
2. Offer free access for honest reviews
3. Create affiliate program (30% commission)
4. Reach out to 5-10 AI newsletter writers

## Day 11-12: Paid Promotion (Optional)
1. Run $50 Facebook/Instagram ad campaign
2. Run $50 Reddit ads in relevant subreddits
3. Test different ad creatives
4. Optimize based on CTR and conversions

## Day 13-14: Optimization
1. Analyze traffic sources
2. A/B test landing page elements
3. Update product descriptions based on feedback
4. Add new prompts based on customer requests

## Day 15: Scale & Repeat
1. Launch second product line
2. Create email sequence for new subscribers
3. Launch affiliate program publicly
4. Plan next 30-day growth strategy

## Revenue Projections
- Conservative: 5 sales/day = $50/day = $1,500/month
- Moderate: 15 sales/day = $150/day = $4,500/month
- Optimistic: 30 sales/day = $300/day = $9,000/month

## Monthly Costs
- Gumroad: Free (5% fee per transaction)
- Domain: ~$12/year
- Hosting: Free (Netlify/Vercel/GitHub Pages)
- Total: Under $15/month

## Profit Margins
- Product cost: $0 (digital goods)
- Platform fee: 5-10%
- Net profit: 90-95%

## Success Metrics
- Day 7: 100 website visits, 1-2 sales

## Tips for Success
1. Focus on quality over quantity in promotions
2. Engage genuinely with communities (no spam)
3. Collect emails from day 1
4. Ask for feedback and iterate quickly
5. Document everything for future products

## Scaling Strategy
1. Launch new prompt packs monthly
2. Create bundle deals
3. Offer customization services
4. License prompts to businesses
5. Create AI-powered prompt generator tool
