50 ChatGPT Prompts for Content Creators: Save Hours Every Week

Published September 9, 2026 · AI Prompt Store Team · 12 min read

Content creators are busy. Between filming, editing, and engaging with your audience, there's rarely time to also write scripts, captions, and emails — and one video can demand a hook, a script, a description, titles, a community post, and four social clips before you touch the next one.

That's why we compiled these 50 tested ChatGPT prompts specifically for creators. They work with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini interchangeably. Each prompt includes the use case and the exact text to copy-paste — and every one is drawn from or expanded on our AI Content Creator Pack, so this is a genuine free preview of the full library.

YouTube Prompts (10)

1. The Hook Generator

Act as a YouTube growth strategist. I'm making a video about [TOPIC]. Write 5 first-3-seconds hooks that stop the scroll: one pattern-interrupt, one curiosity gap, one social proof.

2. Full Script Template

Act as a YouTube scriptwriter. Write a 10-minute script about [TOPIC]. Structure: Hook (0:00-0:30), Problem (0:30-1:30), 3 Solutions (1:30-8:00), CTA (8:00-10:00). Include B-roll and on-screen text notes.

3. Title & Thumbnail Ideas

Act as a YouTube thumbnail designer and title writer. My video is about [TOPIC]. Give me 10 title options (curiosity, list, how-to formats) and 5 clickable thumbnail text ideas.

4. End Screen Script

Act as a YouTube retention expert. Write a 30-second end screen script for my video about [TOPIC]. It should tease 2 other videos and ask viewers to subscribe. Keep it energetic and specific.

5. Community Post Ideas

Act as a YouTube community manager. I just posted a video about [TOPIC]. Write 5 poll ideas and 3 caption options that drive engagement without clickbait.

6. Video Description + SEO

Act as a YouTube SEO specialist. Write a description for my video about [TOPIC]: 2-sentence hook, timestamps for 5 sections, hashtags, and a CTA for my [WEBSITE/NEWSLETTER].

7. Playlist Curation

Act as a YouTube content strategist. I have 12 videos about [NICHE]. Group them into 3 playlists with titles and descriptions that encourage binge-watching.

8. Collab Pitch Template

Act as a YouTube partnership manager. Write a collab pitch to [CREATOR NAME] for a video about [TOPIC]. Short, specific, and mutually beneficial.

9. Live Stream Talking Points

Act as a live stream producer. I'm doing a Q&A stream about [TOPIC]. Write a 1-minute opening monologue, 15 backup questions for slow chat, and a closing segment promoting my next video.

10. Shorts Series Concept

Act as a YouTube Shorts strategist. Turn my long-form video about [TOPIC] into a 5-part Shorts series: hook, visual, and script for each 60-second clip.

Social Media Caption Prompts (15)

11. Instagram Carousel Hook

Act as an Instagram growth specialist. Write a 7-slide carousel about [TOPIC]. Slide 1 stops scrollers, slides 2-6 teach, slide 7 is the CTA.

12. Instagram Reels Script

Act as a social media video producer. Write a 30-second Reels script about [TOPIC]: visual description, on-screen text, voiceover, and trending audio suggestion.

13. Twitter/X Thread Opener

Act as a Twitter/X growth writer. Write a thread opener about [TOPIC] that makes people want to retweet and read the whole thread. Keep it under 280 characters.

14. LinkedIn Post (Professional)

Act as a LinkedIn thought leader. Write a post about [TOPIC] that sparks discussion: a contrarian take plus a question that invites comments.

15. LinkedIn Post (Storytelling)

Act as a LinkedIn storyteller. Write a personal story post about [TOPIC]. Structure: failure, lesson, actionable takeaway. Keep it under 150 words.

16. TikTok Text Overlay Script

Act as a TikTok creator. Write a script about [TOPIC] where text appears on screen as I talk — 5 text overlays plus what I'm saying in each.

17. Instagram Story Sequence

Act as an Instagram Story designer. Create a 5-slide Story sequence about [TOPIC]: 1 poll, 1 quiz sticker, 1 question box, and 2 informational slides.

18. Pinterest Pin Descriptions

Act as a Pinterest SEO specialist. Write 10 Pin descriptions about [TOPIC] — each under 500 characters, keyword-rich, ending with a call-to-action.

19. Threads Post

Act as a Threads creator. Write a casual, conversational post about [TOPIC] that feels like a text to a friend. Under 500 characters.

20. Caption Repurposing

Act as a content repurposing expert. Turn this long-form post [PASTE CONTENT] into: 1 Instagram caption, 1 tweet, 1 LinkedIn blurb, and 1 Facebook update.

21. Hashtag Strategy

Act as a social media strategist. For [TOPIC] content in the [NICHE] niche, give me 15 niche, 10 mid-size, and 5 broad hashtags — and how to rotate them.

22. Engagement-Bait Comment

Act as a social media manager. Write a "first comment" for my post about [TOPIC] that asks a specific, easy-to-answer question to boost engagement.

23. DM Automation Scripts

Act as a customer success rep. Write 3 DM scripts for when someone comments "how do I..." on my [TOPIC] post. Helpful, not salesy.

24. Caption A/B Test

Act as a copywriter. Write 2 versions of an Instagram caption for [TOPIC]. Version A should be question-based. Version B should be statement-based. Keep both under 220 characters.

25. Facebook Group Post

Act as a community manager. Write a post for my Facebook group about [TOPIC]. It should spark discussion and encourage members to share their experiences.

Blog & Newsletter Prompts (10)

26. Blog Post Outline

Act as an SEO content strategist. Create a detailed outline for a 1,500-word post about [TOPIC]: H1, H2/H3 structure, target keywords, and a 1-paragraph brief per section.

27. Newsletter Edition

Act as a newsletter editor. Write a weekly newsletter edition for my [NICHE] audience. Include: 1 industry insight, 1 practical tip, 1 tool recommendation, and 1 question for readers to reply to.

28. Blog Intro That Hooks

Act as a blog writer. Write a 150-word introduction for a blog post about [TOPIC]. Start with a short, relatable story. End with a clear thesis statement.

29. Listicle Generator

Act as a listicle writer. Write a "Top 10" listicle about [TOPIC]. Each item should have: a punchy title, 2-3 sentences explaining why it matters, and 1 actionable tip.

30. Case Study Prompt

Act as a case study writer. Write a 500-word case study about [PRODUCT/SERVICE] for [AUDIENCE]: Challenge, Solution, Results — with specific metrics.

31. Email Sequence (5-Part)

Act as an email marketer. Write a 5-part welcome sequence for new [NICHE] newsletter subscribers — subject lines, preview text, and body copy for each.

32. Product Review Post

Act as an honest product reviewer. Write a 1,000-word review of [PRODUCT] for [AUDIENCE]: pros, cons, best-fit user, and a verdict.

33. FAQ Section

Act as a customer support writer. Write 8 FAQs about [TOPIC/PRODUCT] with clear, friendly answers of 2-3 sentences each.

34. Comment Reply Templates

Act as a community manager. Write 5 templates for common blog comments: praise, criticism, off-topic question, spam, thoughtful disagreement.

35. Guest Post Pitch

Act as a guest blogger. Write a 100-word pitch to [BLOG NAME] for a guest post about [TOPIC]. Include a working title, angle, and why my audience aligns with theirs.

Email & Marketing Prompts (10)

36. Cold Email Template

Act as an outreach specialist. Write a cold email to [PERSON/TITLE] at [COMPANY] wanting to [GOAL]. Under 100 words: 1 personalized line, 1 clear ask, 1 easy "yes" option.

37. Sales Page Copy

Act as a direct-response copywriter. Write sales page copy for [PRODUCT]: headline, 3-bullet benefits, social proof, 3 objection handlers, and CTA button text.

38. Upsell Email

Act as an email marketer. Write an upsell email to buyers of [PRODUCT A] offering [PRODUCT B]. Explain the bundle value; create urgency without being pushy.

39. Re-engagement Campaign

Act as a retention marketer. Write a 3-email re-engagement sequence for subscribers inactive for 60 days. Friendly, slightly playful, no guilt-tripping.

40. Affiliate Promotion

Act as an affiliate marketer. Write a promotional email about [PRODUCT] to my [NICHE] audience: personal story, 3 reasons it's valuable, disclosure, CTA.

41. Lead Magnet Description

Act as a copywriter. Write a landing page description for my lead magnet about [TOPIC]. Include: 3 pain points, 3 benefits, and what they'll get immediately after signing up.

42. Webinar Registration Page

Act as a webinar copywriter. Write registration page copy for a webinar about [TOPIC]: headline, 3 takeaways, speaker bio, date/time, and CTA.

43. Referral Request Email

Act as a customer success rep. Write a polite referral request email to customers who 5-star-reviewed [PRODUCT]: thank you, referral link, and incentive for both sides.

44. Seasonal Campaign

Act as a campaign manager. Write a 3-email Black Friday sequence (announcement, reminder, last call) with subject lines and body copy.

45. Abandoned Cart Recovery

Act as an e-commerce copywriter. Write 2 abandoned cart emails for [PRODUCT]: 1) reminder + social proof, 2) 24-hour urgency + small discount.

Productivity & Planning Prompts (5)

46. Content Calendar

Act as a content strategist. Create a 4-week content calendar for a [NICHE] YouTube channel and Instagram: video topics, thumbnail ideas, caption themes, and posting schedule.

47. Weekly Review

Act as a productivity coach. Write a guided weekly review template for a creator: what went well, what to improve, next week's top 3 priorities, and an energy check-in.

48. Batch Content Session Plan

Act as a content production manager. Plan a 4-hour batch session in 30-minute blocks: film 3 YouTube videos, write 7 captions, edit 1 video, schedule posts.

49. Video Script Repurposing Plan

Act as a content repurposing expert. Create a repurposing plan for my YouTube video about [TOPIC]: 3 Shorts clips, 5 tweets, 1 carousel, 1 blog post, 1 newsletter blurb.

50. Creator Business Audit

Act as a creator business coach. Audit my content business: [CHANNELS, REVENUE, TIME SPENT]. Give me 5 prioritized improvements to increase revenue or save time.
