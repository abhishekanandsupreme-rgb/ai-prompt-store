## Slide 3 — PROMPT 1: WEEKLY PLANNER

**Headline:**
1. Weekly Planner

**Body:**
> Act as an executive assistant.
> My top 3 goals this week: [GOAL 1], [GOAL 2], [GOAL 3].
> My available work hours: [HOURS/DAY].
>
> Create a weekly schedule that:
> - Blocks time for deep work on each goal
> - Includes 30-min buffer between meetings
> - Schedules admin/email for one block only
> - Leaves Friday afternoon for catch-up
>
> Output: Day-by-day schedule with time blocks.

**Visual:**
Calendar icon with blocks and time slots.

---

## Slide 4 — PROMPT 2: PRIORITY FILTER

**Headline:**
2. Priority Filter (Eisenhower Matrix)

**Body:**
> Act as a productivity coach.
> Here's my to-do list: [PASTE 10-15 TASKS].
>
> Categorize each into:
> - Q1: Urgent + Important (do now)
> - Q2: Important, not urgent (schedule)
> - Q3: Urgent, not important (delegate)
> - Q4: Not urgent, not important (delete)
>
> Then tell me: What's the ONE task I should do today?

**Visual:**
2x2 matrix grid with Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 quadrants.

---

## Slide 5 — PROMPT 3: MEETING SUMMARIZER

**Headline:**
3. Meeting Summarizer

**Body:**
> Act as a meeting note expert.
> Here's my raw meeting transcript/notes: [PASTE NOTES].
>
> Turn this into:
> - 3-bullet executive summary
> - Key decisions made
> - Action items (who + what + deadline)
> - Open questions
> - Next meeting agenda (2 items)
>
> Keep it under 300 words.

**Visual:**
Document icon with a checklist overlay.

---

## Slide 6 — PROMPT 4: EMAIL TRIAGE

**Headline:**
4. Email Triage

**Body:**
> Act as an executive assistant.
> Here are 10 emails in my inbox: [PASTE SUBJECTS + 1-SENTENCE CONTENT].
>
> For each email, tell me:
> - Priority: High / Medium / Low
> - Action: Reply / Schedule / Delegate / Archive
> - Draft a 1-sentence reply if high priority

**Visual:**
Inbox icon with a "sort" arrow.

## Slide 7 — PROMPT 5: DAY-END REVIEW

**Headline:**
5. Day-End Review

**Body:**
> Act as a productivity coach.
> Today I accomplished: [LIST 3-5 THINGS].
> Today I struggled with: [LIST BLOCKERS].
>
> Give me:
> 1. A 1-sentence win I should acknowledge
> 2. One thing to improve tomorrow
> 3. Tomorrow's top 3 priorities
> 4. A 2-minute "shutdown ritual" to end the day
