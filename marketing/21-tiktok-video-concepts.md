## PRODUCTION TIPS FOR ALL CONCEPTS

**Filming:**
- Record in vertical (9:16) format
- Good lighting (natural light or ring light)
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Look at camera, not phone screen

**Editing:**
- Fast cuts every 2-3 seconds
- Text overlays for key points
- Zooms on phone screen for prompts
- Trending audio at 15-20% volume
- Captions burned in (most viewers watch without sound)

**Posting Strategy:**
- Post 3x per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)
- Best times: 7-9 AM and 5-7 PM EST
- Engage with first 10 comments within 30 minutes
- Pin a comment with the link and a free bonus
