## The Hidden Cost of "Free"

Free tools often cost more than paid ones when you factor in:

- **Time spent iterating:** 10+ hours per week on prompt tuning
- **Inconsistent results:** Output varies by model update
- **Team friction:** Everyone builds their own prompts, knowledge silos
- **Opportunity cost:** Hours lost to bad output = lost revenue
