# Customer Testimonial Template Pack

Ready-to-use testimonial templates for AI prompt packs. Mix, match, and customize for social proof across every channel.

## Template Structure

Every great testimonial follows this structure:

1. **Before state** — the pain, frustration, or limitation
2. **Discovery** — how they found the solution
3. **Transformation** — specific results, numbers, time saved
4. **Emotion** — how they feel now
5. **Recommendation** — who else should use it
