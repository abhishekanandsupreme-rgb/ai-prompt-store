# 2. 50 ChatGPT Prompts for Content Creators: Save Hours Every Week

**Target Keyword:** ChatGPT prompts for content creators
**Secondary Keywords:** AI for content creation, social media prompts, YouTube script AI prompts
**Meta Description:** 50 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for content creators. Generate YouTube scripts, social media posts, blog outlines, and email sequences in minutes.

---

Content creators are busy. Between filming, editing, and engaging with your audience, there's rarely time to also write scripts, captions, and emails. That's why I compiled 50 tested ChatGPT prompts specifically for creators—so you can offload the writing and focus on the parts of your work that actually matter.

These prompts work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and any other major AI writing tool. Each one includes a specific use case, the exact prompt to copy-paste, and the kind of output you should expect.

## YouTube Video Prompts (10 Prompts)

### 1. The Hook Generator
> Act as a YouTube growth strategist. I'm making a video about [TOPIC]. Write 5 hooks for the first 3 seconds that stop the scroll. Include one pattern-interrupt hook, one curiosity gap hook, and one social proof hook.

### 2. Full Script Template
> Act as a YouTube scriptwriter. Write a 10-minute script for a video about [TOPIC]. Structure: Hook (0:00-0:30), Problem (0:30-1:30), 3 Solutions (1:30-8:00), CTA (8:00-10:00). Include B-roll suggestions and on-screen text notes.

### 3. Title & Thumbnail Ideas
> Act as a YouTube thumbnail designer and title writer. I have a video about [TOPIC]. Give me 10 title options (mix of curiosity, list, and how-to formats) and 5 thumbnail text suggestions that would make someone click.

### 4. End Screen Script
> Act as a YouTube retention expert. Write a 30-second end screen script for my video about [TOPIC]. It should tease 2 other videos and ask viewers to subscribe. Keep it energetic and specific.

### 5. Community Post Ideas
> Act as a YouTube community manager. I just posted a video about [TOPIC]. Write 5 community poll ideas and 3 caption options that drive engagement without being clickbait.

### 6. Video Description + SEO
> Act as an YouTube SEO specialist. Write a video description for my video about [TOPIC]. Include: 2-sentence hook, timestamps for 5 key sections, relevant hashtags, and a call-to-action for my [WEBSITE/NEWSLETTER].

### 7. Playlist Curation
> Act as a YouTube content strategist. I have 12 videos about [NICHE]. Group them into 3 playlists with titles and descriptions that encourage binge-watching.

### 8. Collab Pitch Template
> Act as a YouTube partnership manager. Write a personalized collaboration pitch to [CREATOR NAME] for a video about [TOPIC]. Keep it short, specific, and mutually beneficial.

### 9. Live Stream Talking Points
> Act as a live stream producer. I'm doing a Q&A live stream about [TOPIC]. Write an opening monologue (1 minute), 15 backup questions in case the chat is slow, and a closing segment that promotes my next video.

### 10. Shorts Series Concept
> Act as a YouTube Shorts strategist. Turn my long-form video about [TOPIC] into a 5-part Shorts series. Write the hook, visual, and script for each 60-second clip.

## Social Media Caption Prompts (15 Prompts)

### 11. Instagram Carousel Hook
> Act as an Instagram growth specialist. Write a carousel post about [TOPIC] with 7 slides. Slide 1 should stop scrollers. Slides 2-6 should teach something actionable. Slide 7 should be a CTA.

### 12. Instagram Reels Script
> Act as a social media video producer. Write a 30-second Reels script about [TOPIC]. Include: visual description, on-screen text, voiceover, and trending audio suggestion.

### 13. Twitter/X Thread Opener
> Act as a Twitter/X growth writer. Write a thread opener about [TOPIC] that makes people want to retweet and read the whole thread. Keep it under 280 characters.

### 14. LinkedIn Post (Professional)
> Act as a LinkedIn thought leader. Write a post about [TOPIC] that sparks professional discussion. Include a contrarian take and a question that invites comments.

### 15. LinkedIn Post (Storytelling)
> Act as a LinkedIn storyteller. Write a personal story post about [TOPIC]. Structure: failure, lesson, actionable takeaway. Keep it under 150 words.

### 16. TikTok Text Overlay Script
> Act as a TikTok creator. Write a script for a video about [TOPIC] where the text appears on screen as I talk. Include 5 text overlays and what I'm saying in each.

### 17. Instagram Story Sequence
> Act as an Instagram Story designer. Create a 5-slide Story sequence about [TOPIC]. Include: 1 interactive poll slide, 1 quiz sticker, 1 question box prompt, and 2 informational slides.

### 18. Facebook Group Post
> Act as a community manager. Write a post for my Facebook group about [TOPIC]. It should spark discussion and encourage members to share their experiences.

### 19. Pinterest Pin Descriptions
> Act as a Pinterest SEO specialist. Write 10 Pin descriptions for images about [TOPIC]. Each should be under 500 characters, include keywords, and end with a call-to-action.

### 20. Threads Post
> Act as a Threads creator. Write a casual, conversational post about [TOPIC] that feels like a text to a friend. Under 500 characters.

### 21. Caption Repurposing
> Act as a content repurposing expert. Take this long-form post about [PASTE CONTENT] and turn it into: 1 Instagram caption, 1 tweet, 1 LinkedIn blurb, and 1 Facebook update.

### 22. Hashtag Strategy
> Act as a social media strategist. For content about [TOPIC] in the [NICHE] niche, give me: 15 niche hashtags, 10 mid-size hashtags, and 5 broad hashtags. Explain how to rotate them.

### 23. Engagement-Bait Comment
> Act as a social media manager. Write a "first comment" for my post about [TOPIC] that asks a specific, easy-to-answer question to boost engagement.

### 24. DM Automation Scripts
> Act as a customer success rep. Write 3 DM scripts for when someone comments "how do I..." on my post about [TOPIC]. Make them helpful but not salesy.

### 25. Caption A/B Test
> Act as a copywriter. Write 2 versions of an Instagram caption for [TOPIC]. Version A should be question-based. Version B should be statement-based. Keep both under 220 characters.

## Blog & Newsletter Prompts (10 Prompts)

### 26. Blog Post Outline
> Act as an SEO content strategist. Create a detailed outline for a 1,500-word blog post about [TOPIC]. Include: H1 title, H2/H3 structure, target keywords, and a 1-paragraph description for each section.

### 27. Newsletter Edition
> Act as a newsletter editor. Write a weekly newsletter edition for my [NICHE] audience. Include: 1 industry insight, 1 practical tip, 1 tool recommendation, and 1 question for readers to reply to.

### 28. Blog Intro That Hooks
> Act as a blog writer. Write a 150-word introduction for a blog post about [TOPIC]. Start with a short, relatable story. End with a clear thesis statement.

### 29. Listicle Generator
> Act as a listicle writer. Write a "Top 10" listicle about [TOPIC]. Each item should have: a punchy title, 2-3 sentences explaining why it matters, and 1 actionable tip.

### 30. Case Study Prompt
> Act as a case study writer. Write a 500-word case study about [PRODUCT/SERVICE] for [AUDIENCE]. Structure: Challenge, Solution, Results. Include specific metrics.

### 31. Email Sequence (5-Part)
> Act as an email marketer. Write a 5-part welcome email sequence for new subscribers to my [NICHE] newsletter. Include: subject lines, preview text, and body copy for each email.

### 32. Product Review Post
> Act as an honest product reviewer. Write a 1,000-word review of [PRODUCT] for [AUDIENCE]. Include: pros, cons, who it's best for, and a verdict.

### 33. FAQ Section
> Act as a customer support writer. Write 8 frequently asked questions about [TOPIC/PRODUCT] with clear, friendly answers. Target length: 2-3 sentences each.

### 34. Comment Reply Templates
> Act as a community manager. Write 5 templates for responding to common blog comments: praise, criticism, off-topic question, spam, and thoughtful disagreement.

### 35. Guest Post Pitch
> Act as a guest blogger. Write a 100-word pitch to [BLOG NAME] for a guest post about [TOPIC]. Include a working title, angle, and why my audience aligns with theirs.

## Email & Marketing Prompts (10 Prompts)

### 36. Cold Email Template
> Act as an outreach specialist. Write a cold email to [PERSON/TITLE] at [COMPANY]. I want to [GOAL]. Keep it under 100 words. Include: 1 personalized line, 1 clear ask, 1 easy "yes" option.

### 37. Sales Page Copy
> Act as a direct-response copywriter. Write sales page copy for [PRODUCT]. Include: headline, 3-bullet benefit list, social proof section, 3 objection handlers, and CTA button text.

### 38. Upsell Email
> Act as an email marketer. Write an upsell email to customers who just bought [PRODUCT A]. Offer [PRODUCT B]. Explain the bundle value and create urgency without being pushy.

### 39. Re-engagement Campaign
> Act as a retention marketer. Write a 3-email re-engagement sequence for subscribers who haven't opened my emails in 60 days. Tone: friendly, slightly playful, no guilt-tripping.

### 40. Affiliate Promotion
> Act as an affiliate marketer. Write a promotional email about [PRODUCT] to my [NICHE] audience. Include: personal story, 3 reasons it's valuable, disclosure, and CTA.

### 41. Lead Magnet Description
> Act as a copywriter. Write a landing page description for my lead magnet about [TOPIC]. Include: 3 pain points, 3 benefits, and what they'll get immediately after signing up.

### 42. Webinar Registration Page
> Act as a webinar copywriter. Write the copy for a registration page for a webinar about [TOPIC]. Include: headline, 3 things they'll learn, speaker bio, date/time, and CTA.

### 43. Referral Request Email
> Act as a customer success rep. Write a polite referral request email to customers who gave a 5-star review for [PRODUCT]. Include: thank you, easy referral link, and incentive for both referrer and referee.

### 44. Seasonal Campaign
> Act as a campaign manager. Write a Black Friday email sequence: 3 emails (announcement, reminder, last call). Include subject lines and body copy for each.

### 45. Abandoned Cart Recovery
> Act as an e-commerce copywriter. Write 2 abandoned cart emails for [PRODUCT]. Email 1: reminder + social proof. Email 2: 24-hour urgency + small discount.

## Productivity & Planning Prompts (10 Prompts)

### 46. Content Calendar
> Act as a content strategist. Create a 4-week content calendar for a [NICHE] YouTube channel and Instagram account. Include: video topics, thumbnail ideas, caption themes, and posting schedule.

### 47. Weekly Review
> Act as a productivity coach. Write a guided weekly review template for a content creator. Include: what went well, what to improve, next week's top 3 priorities, and energy management check-in.

### 48. Batch Content Session Plan
> Act as a content production manager. Plan a 4-hour batch content session. Break it into 30-minute blocks for: filming 3 YouTube videos, writing 7 social captions, editing 1 video, and scheduling posts.

### 49. Video Script Repurposing Plan
> Act as a content repurposing expert. Take my YouTube video about [TOPIC] and create a repurposing plan: 3 Shorts clips, 5 tweet ideas, 1 carousel, 1 blog post, and 1 newsletter blurb.

### 50. Creator Business Audit
> Act as a creator business coach. Audit my content creation business: [DESCRIBE YOUR CURRENT SETUP - channels, revenue, time spent]. Give me a prioritized list of 5 improvements that would increase revenue or save time.

## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Copy and paste** the exact prompt text into your AI tool.
2. **Replace the placeholders** (shown in [BRACKETS]) with your specific details.
3. **Iterate:** If the first output isn't perfect, ask the AI to "rewrite this in a more [CASUAL/FORMAL/FUNNY] tone" or "make it shorter/longer."
4. **Save the winners:** When you get a great output, save that prompt as a template for future use.
