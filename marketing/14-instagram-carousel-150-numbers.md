## Slide 7 — WHAT DIDN'T WORK

**Headline:**
What didn't work

**Body:**
❌ Direct "buy my product" posts with no context
❌ Posting once and expecting traction
❌ Pricing too low ($4.99 vs $9.99)
❌ No email capture on landing page

**Lesson:** Context > links. Consistency > viral hits.
