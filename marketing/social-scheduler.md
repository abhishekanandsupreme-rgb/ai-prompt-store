## 4. Engagement Tactics by Platform

**Platform-Specific Tactics:**

- **Spaces:** Host weekly "Prompt Engineering Office Hours" Twitter Spaces.

2. **Personalize connection requests** with a note about their work.
3. **Share** relevant articles with your take in the caption.
4. **Engage with your own post comments** within the first 2 hours (critical for algorithm).

**Platform-Specific Tactics:**
- **Carousels:** LinkedIn’s algorithm loves carousels — they get 3x more engagement than single images.
- **Article posts:** Publish long-form articles directly on LinkedIn for SEO and reach.
- **Tags:** Tag relevant companies, tools, and people (but don’t spam).
- **Polls:** Use LinkedIn Polls in articles to boost dwell time.
- **Newsletter:** Build a LinkedIn Newsletter for weekly prompt tips.
- **Analytics:** Post Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 AM EST for B2B audience.

**Engagement Rate Benchmarks:**

- Great: 4–6% engagement rate

### Detailed Tool Setup Guide

#### Tweetdeck (Twitter/X)
**Cost:** Free  
**URL:** https://tweetdeck.twitter.com

**Setup:**
1. Go to tweetdeck.twitter.com and log in with your Twitter/X account.
2. Click "Add Column" → choose "Scheduled Tweets."
3. Compose tweets in advance and set exact publish times.
4. Use columns for Mentions, Search, and Lists to monitor engagement.

**Workflow:**
- Schedule 1 week of tweets every Sunday.
- Use columns to track real-time mentions and engage.

---

#### Later (Free Tier)
**Cost:** Free (up to 1 social profile per platform, 10 posts per profile)  
**URL:** https://later.com

**Setup:**
1. Sign up with email or social login.
2. Connect Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn profiles.
3. Use the visual calendar to drag-and-drop posts.
4. Set publish times using the "Best Time to Post" suggestions.

**Workflow:**
- Create content in Canva, import directly to Later.
- Schedule 30 posts in advance (10 per platform).
- Use Instagram-first scheduling, then cross-post to TikTok and LinkedIn.

**Limitations & Workarounds:**
- Free tier = 1 profile per platform.  
  → Use a personal LinkedIn profile or create a brand page.
- 10 posts per platform limit.  
  → Prioritize highest-performing content types (Reels > Carousels > Static).

---

#### Canva Scheduler
**Cost:** Free (with Canva account)  
**URL:** https://canva.com

**Setup:**
1. Create designs in Canva (Reels, Carousels, etc.).
2. Click "Share" → "Schedule" on any design.
3. Connect Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.
4. Choose date/time and confirm.

**Workflow:**
- Design all weekly content in Canva on Monday.
- Schedule in batches using the visual calendar.
- Great for visual platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn).

---

#### Meta Business Suite
**Cost:** Free  
**URL:** https://business.facebook.com

**Setup:**
1. Convert Instagram account to a Professional/Creator account.
2. Link Instagram to a Facebook Page.
3. Use Meta Business Suite to schedule posts for both platforms simultaneously.

**Workflow:**
- Schedule Instagram posts and Facebook posts together.
- Use unified inbox for comments and DMs.
- Access basic analytics for both platforms.

---

#### TikTok Creator Portal
**Cost:** Free  
**URL:** https://www.tiktok.com/creators/portal

**Setup:**
1. Go to TikTok Creator Portal → "Schedule video."
2. Upload video, write caption, add hashtags.
3. Set publish date and time.
4. Note: Scheduling is limited to 100 videos in the free tier.

**Workflow:**
- Batch film 10–15 videos on weekends.
- Schedule throughout the week using the Creator Portal.
- Use Later for additional scheduling if you exceed 100 videos.
