## Slide 3 — FRAMEWORK 1: Role + Context + Task

**Headline:**
1. Role + Context + Task

**Body:**
Tell the AI who it is, what it knows, and what you want.

> Act as a [ROLE] with expertise in [DOMAIN].
> Context: [BACKGROUND]
> Task: [SPECIFIC ACTION]

**Example:**
> Act as a senior SaaS copywriter.
> Context: I'm launching a project management tool.
> Task: Write a 5-email welcome sequence.

**Visual:**
Three labeled boxes: Role → Context → Task, with arrows showing flow.

---

## Slide 4 — FRAMEWORK 2: Few-Shot Prompting

**Headline:**
2. Few-Shot Prompting

**Body:**
Show, don't tell. Give 2-3 examples of the output you want.

**Example:**
> Input: "New coffee shop downtown"
> Output: "Your morning just got an upgrade..."
>
> Input: "Yoga studio opening"
> Output: "Stretch into the weekend..."
>
> Now do the same for: "Local bookstore signing"

**Visual:**
Input/Output pairs shown like training data examples.

## Slide 5 — FRAMEWORK 3: Chain-of-Thought

**Headline:**
3. Chain-of-Thought

**Body:**
For complex problems, ask the AI to reason step by step.

> [COMPLEX PROBLEM]
> Before the final answer, walk me through your reasoning. Think about [FACTORS] and explain your logic.

**Result:**
You get transparent, auditable reasoning instead of a random guess.

**Visual:**
Step-by-step flow diagram: Question → Reasoning → Answer.

---

## Slide 6 — FRAMEWORK 4: Constraint + Format

**Headline:**
4. Constraint + Format

**Body:**
Give the AI guardrails and a clear output structure.

**Example:**
> Write 5 subject lines.
> Constraints:
> - Under 50 characters
> - Include emoji in 3
> - No spam words
> - Curiosity-driven
> Output: Numbered list + 1-sentence explanation

**Visual:**
A "constraints box" and an "output format" box side by side.

---

## Slide 7 — FRAMEWORK 5: Self-Review

**Headline:**
5. Self-Review Prompt

**Body:**
After getting a response, ask the AI to critique its own work.

> Review your response:
> 1. What's missing?
> 2. Where can I add specificity?
> 3. What objections might a reader have?
>
> Then rewrite addressing these.

**Result:**
B+ output becomes A+ output. Every time.

**Visual:**
Before/after comparison: Draft → Critique → Final Version.

## Slide 9 — POWER PROMPT EXAMPLE

**Headline:**
Combine them all for a power prompt

**Body:**
> Act as a direct-response copywriter (Role).
> Context: I sell AI prompts on Gumroad (Context).
> Task: Write a 1,500-word blog post (Task).
>
> Example style: [PASTE SAMPLE] (Few-Shot)
> Before final draft, outline and explain choices (Chain-of-Thought)
> Constraints: Every section needs examples, no generic advice (Constraint + Format)
> Then critique your own draft (Self-Review)
