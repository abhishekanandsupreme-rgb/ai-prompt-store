## [8:45-9:30] BONUS TIP: How to Make AI Content Sound Like YOU

**[VISUAL: Host back on camera, serious tone.]**

**HOST:**
Here's the thing. If you use these prompts and don't edit, your content will sound generic. AI has tells—specific phrases, sentence structures, and rhythms that are dead giveaways.

So here's my editing process:

1. **Read it out loud.** If you stumble, your audience will tune out.
2. **Cut 20%.** AI writes with too many words. Humans write with purpose.
3. **Add one specific detail.** A number, a name, a story. Anything that proves you lived it.
4. **Check for AI phrases.** "Delve into," "multifaceted," "in today's digital landscape"—delete them all.

Do this, and nobody will know AI touched your content.
