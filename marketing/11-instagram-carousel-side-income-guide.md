## Slide 5 — STEP 3: SET UP GUMROAD (30 MIN)

**Headline:**
Step 3: Set up Gumroad

**Body:**
Gumroad is the fastest way to sell digital products.

1. Sign up (free)
2. Create a new product
3. Upload your file
4. Write title, description, and tags
5. Set price ($9.99 - $29.99 is the sweet spot)
6. Publish

Fees: 10% on free plan, 5% on paid plan ($10/mo).
