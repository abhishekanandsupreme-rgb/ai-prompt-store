## DESIGN NOTES

**Color Palette:**
- Primary background: #0F0F0F (dark)
- Text: #FFFFFF (white)
- Accent: #3B82F6 (blue)
- Secondary accent: #10B981 (green)
- CTA button: #3B82F6 with hover #2563EB

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Inter Bold, 48-64px
- Body: Inter Regular, 18-20px
- Buttons: Inter SemiBold, 18px
- Trust badges: Inter Medium, 14px

**Layout:**
- Single column, centered, max-width 720px
- Generous spacing between sections (80-120px)
- Sticky CTA button on mobile
- Social proof bar immediately below hero

**Mobile Optimization:**
- Hero CTA button full-width on mobile
- Testimonial cards stack vertically
- FAQ accordion (click to expand)
- Email input and button stacked

**Integrations:**
- Email provider: Buttondown / Beehiiv / ConvertKit
- Thank you page: Redirect to download page with PDF
- Thank you email: Send immediately with download link
- Welcome sequence: 5-email drip over 2 weeks
