## Slide 3 — INSTAGRAM CAPTION PROMPT

**Headline:**
Instagram Caption Prompt

**Body:**
> Act as an Instagram engagement specialist.
> Post type: [REEL / CAROUSEL / PHOTO]
> Topic: [TOPIC]
> Audience: [AUDIENCE]
>
> Write 3 caption options:
> 1. Storytelling (personal anecdote)
> 2. Educational (3 actionable tips)
> 3. Engagement bait (easy question)
>
> Rules:
> - Under 220 words
> - End with a specific question
> - Include 5-10 hashtags
> - No "link in bio" unless it's the CTA

**Visual:**
Phone mockup showing an Instagram post with comments.

## Slide 4 — TWITTER/THREAD PROMPT

**Headline:**
Twitter/X Thread Prompt

**Body:**
> Act as a Twitter growth writer.
> Topic: [TOPIC]
> Audience: [AUDIENCE]
>
> Write a thread:
> - Hook tweet (under 280 chars, pattern interrupt)
> - 4-5 body tweets (one idea per tweet)
> - Closing tweet (CTA + question)
>
> Rules:
> - Use short sentences
> - Include one "this is controversial but..." tweet
> - Add a specific number or stat in tweet 2

**Visual:**
Twitter thread layout with numbered tweets.

## Slide 5 — LINKEDIN POST PROMPT

**Headline:**
LinkedIn Post Prompt

**Body:**
> Act as a LinkedIn thought leader.
> Topic: [TOPIC]
> Industry: [INDUSTRY]
>
> Write 2 versions:
> 1. Professional take (contrarian opinion + data)
> 2. Storytelling (personal failure → lesson → takeaway)
>
> Rules:
> - Under 150 words
> - No hashtags
> - End with a question that invites discussion
> - Tone: Conversational but authoritative

LinkedIn post mockup with "see more" link.

## Slide 6 — TIKTOK/REELS SCRIPT PROMPT

**Headline:**
TikTok/Reels Script Prompt

**Body:**
> Act as a short-form video producer.
> Topic: [TOPIC]
> Length: 30 seconds
> Audience: [AUDIENCE]
>
> Write a script:
> - Hook (first 3 seconds, text overlay)
> - Body (what I say + what appears on screen)
> - CTA (last 3 seconds)
>
> Include: trending audio suggestion, text overlays, and a caption option.

## Slide 9 — THE SECRET SAUCE

**Headline:**
The secret sauce: specific details

**Body:**
Generic prompts get generic engagement.

Specific prompts get specific engagement.

**Instead of:**
"Write a caption about productivity."

**Write:**
"Write a caption about how I stopped checking email first thing in the morning and gained 90 minutes of deep work. Include a specific stat."

Specificity = relatability = engagement.
