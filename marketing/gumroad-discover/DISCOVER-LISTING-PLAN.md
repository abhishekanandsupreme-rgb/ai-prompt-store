## 1. Verified Discover mechanics (checked Sep 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| A product lists on Discover only if: category selected (Share tab), ratings enabled, and **≥1 successful sale** (refunded sales don't count) | Gumroad Help #79 |
| Risk-team verification runs **~3 weeks on average after you pass the $100 threshold** — this is the human review gate before Discover traffic flows | Gumroad Help #79 |
| Fees: **10% + $0.50 per direct sale; 30% on Discover marketplace sales** | gumroad.com/pricing |
| Discover products are **auto-opted into Gumroad Affiliates** (you can opt out) | Gumroad Help #79 |
| Tags render as **filter chips inside each category** — they drive in-category filtering, so they must match how buyers actually filter | Gumroad Help #79 |
| Discover submission/effectiveness is **per-product** — one bad category choice only hurts one product | Gumroad Help #79 |

Expect Discover to switch on around week 3–4 after the $100 threshold + risk review, which is *mid-pricing-test* — handled in `PRICING-TEST-PLAN.md`.

## 7. What NOT to do

- **Don't dump all 11 into one category** — they'd cannibalize each other's in-category ranking and share one tag-filter pool.
- **Don't pick Design for anything** to "get coverage" — no product here is a design product; miscategorization is the #1 reason human reviewers bounce a listing.
- **Don't change categories mid-pricing-test** — it resets the product's Discover context while you're measuring price. Category changes wait until Day 36+.
- **Don't rely on Discover for week-1 revenue** — the 30% fee plus the risk-review lag means week 1 is 100% owned traffic.
