# 5. AI vs. Human Copywriting: When to Use Prompts and When to Write Yourself

**Target Keyword:** AI vs human copywriting
**Secondary Keywords:** AI copywriting pros and cons, when to use AI for writing, AI content quality
**Meta Description:** AI vs human copywriting isn't a competition—it's a workflow. Learn exactly when to use AI prompts and when to write copy yourself for best results.

---

The debate about AI copywriting vs. human writing is missing the point. The question isn't whether AI will replace copywriters. The question is: how do you combine the strengths of both to get better results, faster?

After using AI prompts for copywriting across landing pages, email sequences, social media, and long-form content, I've developed a clear framework for when to delegate to AI and when to keep the keyboard for yourself.

## The Strengths of AI Copywriting

AI excels at:
- **Volume:** Generating 10 headline variations in 10 seconds
- **Pattern matching:** Applying proven copywriting formulas consistently
- **Structure:** Organizing thoughts into AIDA, PAS, or BAB frameworks
- **Research synthesis:** Summarizing customer reviews, competitor pages, or market data
- **First drafts:** Getting words on the page when you're stuck

## The Strengths of Human Copywriting

Humans excel at:
- **Empathy:** Understanding nuanced customer emotions
- **Brand voice:** Infusing personality that feels authentic, not generated
- **Cultural context:** Knowing what's actually funny, relevant, or timely
- **Risk assessment:** Avoiding tone-deaf or legally risky messaging
- **Strategic editing:** Cutting fluff and amplifying what actually sells

## The Workflow: How to Combine Both

### Use AI for: The First Draft (80% of the work)

**When:** You know what you want to say but need help structuring and expanding.

**Prompt template:**
> Act as a direct-response copywriter.
> Write a landing page headline and 3-bullet benefit list for [PRODUCT].
> Audience: [AUDIENCE PERSONA]
> Main benefit: [PRIMARY TRANSFORMATION]
> Tone: [BRAND VOICE - e.g., professional, playful, authoritative]
>
> Output format:
> - Headline
> - Subheadline
> - 3 benefit bullets (each with "feature + emotional payoff")

**Why this works:** AI gives you options fast. You pick the best direction and refine.

### Use AI for: Pattern Application

**When:** You have a winning formula and need to apply it across multiple pages or campaigns.

**Prompt template:**
> Here is a high-performing landing page that converts at [X]%:
> [PASTE WINNING PAGE]
>
> Now write a new landing page for [NEW PRODUCT] using the EXACT same structure, flow, and persuasion patterns. Keep the writing style, CTA placement, and psychological triggers identical.

**Why this works:** AI is exceptional at pattern replication. You provide the pattern, AI provides the variation.

### Use Humans for: The Emotional Core

**When:** The copy needs to sound like a real person telling a real story.

**Prompt template:**
> Act as a writing coach. Here's my draft story about [TOPIC]:
> [PASTE DRAFT]
>
> Critique this for: authenticity, emotional resonance, and specific detail. Identify any parts that feel generic or "AI-written" and suggest specific rewrites that sound human.

**Why this works:** AI can write a story, but humans know when it feels fake. Use AI to draft, use human judgment to edit for truth.

### Use AI for: SEO and Technical Optimization

**When:** You need to hit keyword targets or format content for search.

**Prompt template:**
> Optimize this landing page copy for the primary keyword "[KEYWORD]" and secondary keywords "[KEYWORD 2, KEYWORD 3]".
> Requirements:
> - Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, and 1 subheading
> - Natural keyword density (1-2%)
> - Meta description under 160 characters
> - No keyword stuffing
>
> Copy to optimize:
> [PASTE COPY]

**Why this works:** AI can count words and check placement better than most humans.

### Use Humans for: Final Polish and Proofreading

**When:** The AI draft is good but needs a human touch.

**Tasks humans should always do:**
- Read the copy out loud (AI often uses awkward rhythm)
- Check for brand voice consistency
- Verify factual claims and legal compliance
- Test CTAs for clarity and urgency
- Remove any AI telltale phrases ("delve into," "multifaceted," "in today's digital landscape")

## The "Red Flag" Test: Is This AI Copy?

Here are 5 signs your copy needs a human rewrite:

1. **Every sentence is the same length** — Humans write with rhythm; AI writes with uniformity.
2. **No specific numbers or dates** — "Save time" vs. "Save 4.5 hours per week."
3. **The CTA feels generic** — "Learn more" vs. "Get the 3-part video training."
4. **Zero personality** — No humor, no opinions, no risks taken.
5. **Missing the "so what?"** — Every feature needs an emotional payoff. AI often states features without explaining why they matter.

## Case Study: Landing Page A/B Test

I tested AI-written vs. human-refined copy on a product page.

**Version A (AI only):**
- Written entirely by ChatGPT using a prompt
- Clean structure, all benefits listed
- Converted at 2.3%

**Version B (AI draft + human edit):**
- Same AI-generated first draft
- Human rewrote the headline and first paragraph
- Cut 40% of the copy
- Added 2 specific customer quotes
- Converted at 3.8%

**Result:** The human edit increased conversions by 65%. The AI did the heavy lifting; the human did the selling.

## When NOT to Use AI for Copywriting

| Situation | Why AI Struggles | What to Do Instead |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| Brand voice development | AI doesn't know your audience's slang, inside jokes, or values | Write your brand voice guide yourself, then use prompts to apply it |
| Crisis communications | AI doesn't understand nuance, liability, or public sentiment | Human only—no exceptions |
| Legal/medical copy | Accuracy and compliance are non-negotiable | Human expert + AI for structure |
| Highly emotional messaging | Grief, celebration, and empathy require human experience | Human-first, AI-assisted for structure |
| First-ever brand messaging | No existing patterns to learn from | Human strategy, AI for execution |

## The 80/20 Rule for AI Copywriting

- **80% of the time:** Use AI for first drafts, volume generation, and pattern application.
- **20% of the time:** Use humans for emotional core, brand voice, and final polish.

The teams that win are the ones that stop arguing about "AI vs. human" and start building workflows that use both.

## My Favorite Copywriting Prompt

> Act as a legendary direct-response copywriter (think Gary Halbert or Joanna Wiebe).
> Write [CONTENT TYPE] for [PRODUCT].
>
> Rules:
> 1. Lead with the customer's pain, not your product's features
> 2. Use specific numbers, not vague claims
> 3. Write at an 8th-grade reading level
> 4. End every section with a micro-commitment (question, CTA, or pattern interrupt)
> 5. Remove any word that doesn't drive the reader to the next sentence
>
> Topic: [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU'RE SELLING]
