## Slide 3 — PROMPT 1: SUBJECT LINE GENERATOR

**Headline:**
1. Subject Line Generator

**Body:**
> Act as a direct-response email copywriter.
> Email topic: [TOPIC]
> Audience: [AUDIENCE]
> Goal: [OPEN / CLICK / REPLY]
>
> Write 10 subject lines mixing:
> - 3 curiosity gaps
> - 3 list/numbers
> - 2 social proof
> - 2 personal/emotional
>
> Constraints:
> - Under 50 characters
> - No spam words (free, act now, limited time)
> - No ALL CAPS
> - Include emoji in 3

## Slide 5 — PROMPT 3: WELCOME EMAIL

**Headline:**
3. Welcome Email (The Most Important One)

**Body:**
> Act as a customer success rep.
> Lead magnet: [WHAT THEY DOWNLOADED]
> Brand voice: [TONE]
>
> Write a welcome email:
> - Deliver the lead magnet clearly
> - Set expectations (what emails they'll get, how often)
> - Share one quick win related to the lead magnet
> - Introduce yourself briefly
> - Soft CTA: follow on [SOCIAL] or reply with a question

## Slide 7 — PROMPT 5: ABANDONED CART / RE-ENGAGEMENT

**Headline:**
5. Re-Engagement Email

**Body:**
> Act as a retention marketer.
> Context: [SUBSCRIBERS WHO HAVEN'T OPENED IN 60 DAYS / CART ABANDONERS]
> Product: [PRODUCT]
>
> Write 3 emails:
> 1. Friendly check-in (no guilt)
> 2. Value reminder (what they're missing)
> 3. Last-chance incentive (small discount or bonus)
>
> Rules:
> - Tone: Warm, slightly playful
> - No guilt-tripping language
> - Include an easy "unsubscribe" or "take me off" option

**Visual:**
Cart icon with a "come back" message.

## Slide 8 — PROMPT 6: COLD OUTREACH EMAIL

**Headline:**
6. Cold Outreach Email

**Body:**
> Act as an outreach specialist.
> Recipient: [PERSON/TITLE] at [COMPANY]
> My goal: [ASK]
> Common ground: [SHARED INTEREST / MUTUAL CONNECTION]
>
> Write an email:
> - Subject line (personalized, not salesy)
> - 100-word body max
> - 1 personalized line showing I did research
> - 1 clear ask
> - 1 easy "yes" option
>
> No attachments. No HTML. Plain text.
