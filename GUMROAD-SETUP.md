## 2. Step-by-Step Gumroad Signup

Do NOT use automation. Follow these steps manually in your browser.

### Step 1 — Create Account

1. Go to **https://gumroad.com**
2. Click **Sign up** (top right)
3. Enter your email address and choose a password
4. Check your inbox for the verification email
5. Click the verification link inside
6. You are now logged in to your Gumroad dashboard

### Step 2 — Complete Profile

1. Click your avatar → **Settings**
2. Under **Profile**, fill in:
   - **Name:** Your full name or brand name (e.g., "AI Prompt Store")
   - **Bio:** 1–2 sentences about you/your brand
   - **Profile picture:** Upload a clear headshot or logo (recommended: 400×400 px)
3. Scroll to **Payouts** → Add your preferred payout method:
   - **Bank transfer** (direct deposit) — lower fees, 1–2 business days
   - **PayPal** — instant, slightly higher fees
4. Complete the **Tax information** section:
   - If US-based: fill W-9
   - If non-US: fill W-8BEN (no US tax withholding)
5. Save all settings

### Step 3 — Verify Payout Method

1. Gumroad will send a small verification deposit (or require micro-deposit confirmation)
2. Confirm in Settings → Payouts once received
3. Minimum payout threshold: $10 (varies by country)

### Step 4 — Configure Store Preferences

1. Settings → **General**
   - Set **Currency:** USD
   - Set **Locale:** United States (or your region)
2. Settings → **Emails**
   - Verify the **seller notification email** — you want to know when a sale happens
   - Optional: Turn on **product update emails** to notify customers of new prompts
3. Settings → **Advanced**
   - Enable **Two-factor authentication** (strongly recommended)
   - Review **refund policy** — default is 30 days; consider 14 days for digital products

## 7. Troubleshooting Tips

### Account & Payout Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't verify email | Check spam folder; resend verification from Settings |
| Payout not received | Minimum is $10; bank transfers take 1–2 business days; PayPal is instant |
| Account flagged | Ensure products are original; Gumroad reviews new sellers randomly |
| Tax form rejected | Use exact legal name matching government ID |

### Product Upload Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Upload fails / times out | Reduce file size; ZIP files over 100 MB can fail (yours are ~8 KB — fine) |
| File corrupts after download | Re-upload; ensure you uploaded the ZIP, not the extracted folder |
| Wrong file delivered | Double-check the file attached to each product in dashboard |
| Description formatting broken | Use plain text; avoid fancy markdown — Gumroad renders basic HTML only |

### Checkout & Sales Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Something went wrong" at checkout | Buyer's bank declined; ask them to use PayPal or different card |
| No email confirmation sent | Check spam folder; verify sender email in Settings → Emails |
| Refund request | Process from dashboard → Orders; Gumroad refunds fee too |
| Duplicate charges | Rare; usually a bank authorization hold, not a real charge |

### SEO & Discovery Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Product not showing in search | Fill all tags and category; write a full description (200+ words) |
| Low conversion rate | Improve cover image; add social proof (ratings); test new description |
| No organic traffic | Share on 3+ platforms daily; build backlinks from blog posts |

### File & Content Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Prompts have typos | Edit the `.md` file locally, re-zip for bundle, re-upload both |
| Missing prompts in bundle | Re-create ZIP from `products/` folder: `zip -r bundle-all-10-packs.zip products/prompt-pack-*` |
| Customer asks for new format | Offer PDF/Markdown as a free update; Gumroad notifies all buyers automatically |

## 8. Revenue & Operations

### Daily Operations (15 min/day)

1. Check new sales in Gumroad dashboard
2. Respond to any customer emails (Gumroad forwards them)
3. Post 1 piece of content (tweet, Reddit comment, LinkedIn post)
4. Log daily revenue in your tracking sheet

### Weekly Operations (1 hour/week)

1. Review which products sell best
2. Update underperforming product descriptions
3. Add 5–10 new prompts to top-selling packs
4. Re-upload updated files — Gumroad notifies existing buyers of updates (free marketing)

### Monthly Operations (2 hours/month)

1. Analyze traffic sources (Google Analytics if connected)
2. A/B test one product title or cover image
3. Launch 1 new prompt pack or bonus
4. Review payout history and plan next month's budget

### Tax & Legal

- **Income reporting:** Gumroad sends a 1099 if you earn >$600/year (US sellers)
- **Sales tax:** Gumroad automatically collects and remits in most jurisdictions
- **Refunds:** Keep refund rate under 5% to avoid account review
- **Content policy:** Ensure all prompts are original or properly licensed

### Scaling Beyond Gumroad

Once you hit $500/month:
1. Move hosting to your own domain with Gumroad overlay checkout
2. Add an email list (ConvertKit, Beehiiv) to reduce platform dependency
3. Launch on other marketplaces (Etsy, Creative Market, Lemonsqueezy)
4. Create a membership ($9/month) with monthly new prompts
