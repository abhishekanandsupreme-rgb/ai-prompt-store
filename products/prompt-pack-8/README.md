## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("find keywords for my niche"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, the exact tools and filter setups, decision criteria stated as thresholds, honest-note requirements (what SEO can't promise, what's myth), and verification steps baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS] with your real site and data, paste, and get specialist-level analysis.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — real keywords, real URLs, real GSC numbers. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Verify with real data — the prompts tell you what to check in GSC, logs, and crawlers.
