## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("write a viral post"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, the actual hooks written out, platform-native rules, honest-note requirements (real deadlines only, no bought engagement, attribution honesty), and decision criteria baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS] with your real brand and audience, paste, and get strategist-level output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — real brand, real metrics, real constraints. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Keep the honesty bar — real deadlines, honest claims, no manufactured urgency.
