# AI Real Estate Prompt Pack - 40 Expert Prompts

> How to use: Copy any prompt below, replace the [BRACKETED] variables with your details, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Each prompt includes a role, context, task, and output format for professional-grade results.

## Listings & Descriptions

1. "You are a luxury real estate copywriter. Write a 300-word property description for a [BEDROOM]-bed, [BATH]-bath [PROPERTY TYPE: single-family, condo, townhome] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Highlight [KEY FEATURES: e.g., chef's kitchen, fenced yard, downtown views]. Target audience: [BUYER PERSONA]. Format: opening hook, feature walkthrough, lifestyle close - no clichés like 'must-see' or 'nestled'."
2. "Act as a social media real estate marketer. Create 10 Instagram caption variations for a just-listed property at [ADDRESS]. Include emojis, neighborhood callouts, and a clear CTA to schedule a showing. Format: caption list with hashtag set per caption (mix of neighborhood, city, and lifestyle tags)."
3. "You are a listing optimization specialist. Write a compelling real estate listing headline and 25-word teaser for a [PROPERTY TYPE] in [CITY]. Optimize for Zillow, Realtor.com, and social media. Format: 5 headline options (character counts noted), plus the teaser, plus the keyword logic for each platform."
4. "Act as a pre-marketing strategist. Draft a 'Coming Soon' teaser campaign for a luxury estate in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Include email subject lines, social posts, and direct mail copy. Format: 10-day countdown campaign calendar with the asset per day (email, post, mailer) and the exclusivity framing."
5. "You are a short-term rental copywriter. Write a short-term rental (STR) property description for [AIRBNB/VRBO] optimized for SEO in [CITY]. Include nearby attractions, target guest persona, and booking call-to-action. Format: title (under 50 characters), description with attraction names woven in, and the amenity summary."
6. "You are a property description A/B specialist. My listing for [ADDRESS] has [VIEWS] views and [SAVES] saves but few showing requests. Diagnose the listing: photo order and lead image, description hook weakness, headline keywords, and price-position mismatch. Give 3 rewritten versions of the weakest element and the test plan."
7. "Act as a commercial real estate marketer. Write the marketing package copy for a [PROPERTY TYPE: office, retail, warehouse] at [ADDRESS]: the investment highlights, the tenant/lease summary, the market positioning, and the call-for-offers language. Format: OM (offering memorandum) section structure with the executive summary first."
8. "You are a listing photo director. Plan the photo shot list for [PROPERTY TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]: the 12 must-have shots (hero exterior, living, kitchen, primary suite, baths, outdoor, neighborhood), the time-of-day and lighting notes, the staging prep per room, and the drone/3D tour decision. Format: room-by-room shot brief for the photographer."

## Market Analysis & Reports

9. "You are a comparative market analysis expert. Generate a comparative market analysis (CMA) structure for [ADDRESS] using recent comps within 1 mile and 6 months. Adjust for [FEATURES: square footage, lot size, renovations]. Provide recommended list price range. Format: comp table with adjustment column, then price conclusion with confidence notes."
10. "Act as a market research analyst. Write a neighborhood market report for [AREA] covering median sale price, days on market, inventory levels, price per square foot, and absorption rate for [TIME PERIOD]. Format: metric dashboard table, then 3 takeaways for buyers and 3 for sellers."
11. "You are an investment analyst. Create an investor-ready market snapshot for [CITY/METRO] highlighting population growth, job growth, rental yield averages, and cap rate trends for multifamily properties. Format: market snapshot one-pager with data table and the 'why invest now/why be cautious' sections."
12. "Act as a buy-and-hold investment advisor. Analyze the pros and cons of investing in [PROPERTY TYPE] in [MARKET] for a buy-and-hold investor. Include cash-on-cash return, CapEx reserves, and exit strategy considerations. Format: returns table with the conservative/base/aggressive assumption sets."
13. "You are a market commentary writer. Draft a real estate market commentary newsletter for [AUDIENCE: buyers, sellers, investors] summarizing current conditions and giving 3 actionable recommendations. Format: newsletter with data summary, narrative, recommendations, and the disclaimer footer."
14. "Act as a housing market forecaster. Assess the 12-month outlook for [MARKET]: the supply indicators (permits, inventory trend, months of supply), demand indicators (migration, employment, mortgage rates effect), the price scenarios (softening, flat, growth), and what would change the forecast. Format: scenario table with probability estimates and honest uncertainty notes."
15. "You are a relocation specialist. Build the relocation guide for someone moving to [CITY] from [ORIGIN]: the top 5 neighborhoods for [BUYER PROFILE: young professional, family, retiree] with median prices, the commute and school notes, the cost-of-living comparison, and the 30-day moving timeline. Format: guide structure with neighborhood comparison table."
16. "Act as a real estate data analyst. My farm area [NEIGHBORHOOD] data: [PASTE: sales, days on market, price trends]. Find the patterns: which property types move fastest, the seasonality, the price band with the most activity, and the listing opportunities (owners of [PROPERTY CHARACTERISTIC] likely to sell). Format: analysis with the action list for my farm campaign."

23. "You are a maintenance operations designer. 24. "Act as a fair housing compliance advisor.
