# AI Real Estate Prompt Pack - 20 Prompts

## Listings & Descriptions
1. "Write a 300-word property description for a [BEDROOM]-bed, [BATH]-bath [PROPERTY TYPE: single-family, condo, townhome] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Highlight [KEY FEATURES: e.g., chef's kitchen, fenced yard, downtown views]. Target audience: [BUYER PERSONA]."
2. "Create 10 Instagram caption variations for a just-listed property at [ADDRESS]. Include emojis, neighborhood callouts, and a clear CTA to schedule a showing."
3. "Write a compelling real estate listing headline and 25-word teaser for a [PROPERTY TYPE] in [CITY]. Optimize for Zillow, Realtor.com, and social media."
4. "Draft a 'Coming Soon' teaser campaign for a luxury estate in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Include email subject lines, social posts, and direct mail copy."
5. "Write a short-term rental (STR) property description for [AIRBNB/VRBO] optimized for SEO in [CITY]. Include nearby attractions, target guest persona, and booking call-to-action."

## Market Analysis & Reports
6. "Generate a comparative market analysis (CMA) for [ADDRESS] using recent comps within 1 mile and 6 months. Adjust for [FEATURES: square footage, lot size, renovations]. Provide recommended list price range."
7. "Write a neighborhood market report for [AREA] covering median sale price, days on market, inventory levels, price per square foot, and absorption rate for [TIME PERIOD]."
8. "Create an investor-ready market snapshot for [CITY/METRO] highlighting population growth, job growth, rental yield averages, and cap rate trends for multifamily properties."
9. "Analyze the pros and cons of investing in [PROPERTY TYPE] in [MARKET] for a buy-and-hold investor. Include cash-on-cash return, CapEx reserves, and exit strategy considerations."
10. "Draft a real estate market commentary newsletter for [AUDIENCE: buyers, sellers, investors] summarizing current conditions and giving 3 actionable recommendations."

## Leases & Property Management
11. "Write a residential lease agreement for a [BEDROOM]-bedroom unit at [ADDRESS] in [STATE]. Include rent amount, security deposit, pet policy, maintenance responsibilities, and default remedies."
12. "Create a lease renewal offer letter for [TENANT NAME] at [ADDRESS]. Include new rent amount, lease term, any concessions, and response deadline."
13. "Draft a move-in/move-out inspection checklist for a [PROPERTY TYPE]. Include room-by-room condition notes, photo requirements, and security deposit disposition language."
14. "Write a rent increase notice for [TENANT NAME] at [ADDRESS] compliant with [STATE] notice requirements. Include effective date, new rent amount, and tenant options."
15. "Create a property management agreement for [OWNER NAME] covering [PROPERTY ADDRESS]. Include management fee, leasing fee, maintenance authority, reporting cadence, and termination clause."

## Buyer & Seller Services
16. "Write a buyer consultation script for first-time homebuyers in [CITY]. Cover pre-approval, wish list definition, neighborhood tour strategy, offer process, and inspection expectations."
17. "Create a home preparation checklist for a seller listing at [ADDRESS]. Include staging tips, minor repairs, professional photography, and pre-listing inspection recommendations."
18. "Draft an offer letter template for [BUYER NAME] purchasing a property at [ADDRESS] for $[OFFER PRICE]. Include earnest money, contingencies, closing timeline, and personal touch paragraph."
19. "Write a post-closing client follow-up sequence for a buyer who just closed on [ADDRESS]. Include 3 emails over 90 days covering home maintenance, neighborhood tips, and referral ask."
20. "Create a listing presentation outline for [SELLER NAME] at [ADDRESS]. Include CMA, marketing plan, staging recommendations, comparable days on market, and commission justification."
