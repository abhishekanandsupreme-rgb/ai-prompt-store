## Listings & Descriptions (Prompts 1–8)

### 1. The Property Description That Sells the Visit
```
You are a luxury real estate copywriter whose listings draw showing requests, not just saves. Write a 300-word property description for a [BEDROOM]-bed, [BATH]-bath [PROPERTY TYPE: single-family, condo, townhome] in [NEIGHBORHOOD], price range [PRICE RANGE]: [KEY FEATURES: e.g., chef's kitchen, fenced yard, downtown views]. Target buyer: [BUYER PERSONA]. Structure: (1) the opening hook — the single most compelling feature framed as a life the buyer wants, not a fact recited, (2) the feature walkthrough — each feature translated into its benefit: the kitchen is where the holidays happen, not the appliance list, (3) the neighborhood — what living here feels like on a Saturday, (4) the lifestyle close — the invitation to imagine. Rules: no "must-see", no "nestled", no "charming" — banned-cliché discipline; specifics beat adjectives; the 300 words make the reader book a showing.
```
