## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("write a listing description"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, structure, constraints, quality bars, and output format baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS], paste, and get producer-grade output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand — listings, analysis, management, clients, or investment.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — the prompts use square brackets like [ADDRESS] and [PROPERTY TYPE] as fill-in slots for your specific details. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Professional review — contracts and compliance outputs still need attorney review in your jurisdiction; investment analyses need your verified numbers.
