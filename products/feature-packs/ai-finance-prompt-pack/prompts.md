# AI Finance Prompt Pack - 40 Expert Prompts

> How to use: Copy any prompt below, replace the [BRACKETED] variables with your details, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Each prompt includes a role, context, task, and output format for professional-grade results.

## Financial Analysis & Reporting

1. "You are a senior financial analyst. Analyze the financial statements for [COMPANY NAME] for the period [DATE RANGE]. Calculate and interpret revenue growth, gross margin, operating margin, ROE, and current ratio. Identify 3 strengths and 3 risks. Format: ratio table with 2-year comparison, then findings."
2. "Act as an FP&A manager. Write a variance analysis report comparing actual results vs. budget for [DEPARTMENT/PRODUCT] in [PERIOD]. Explain key drivers of favorable and unfavorable variances. Format: summary table of top 5 variances, then narrative per variance with the 'so what' for next quarter."
3. "You are a cash flow forecaster. Create a 12-month cash flow forecast for [BUSINESS MODEL: SaaS, e-commerce, services]. Include operating, investing, and financing activities. Highlight potential cash shortfalls and mitigation strategies. Format: monthly cash table, shortfall flags, and 3 mitigation levers ranked by speed."
4. "Act as a valuation analyst. Build a DCF valuation model for [COMPANY] with assumptions for revenue growth, EBITDA margin, WACC, and terminal growth rate. Show sensitivity analysis for ±10% changes in WACC and growth. Format: assumptions table, valuation bridge, and sensitivity matrix."
5. "You are an investment committee analyst. Write an investment memorandum for [PROJECT/ACQUISITION] covering thesis, market size, financial projections, risks, and recommended allocation. Length: 3 pages. Format: one-page executive summary first, then sections with supporting tables."

## Budgeting & Forecasting

6. "You are a budgeting specialist. Create a zero-based budget template for a [DEPARTMENT: marketing, engineering, operations] team. Include headcount, software, campaigns, and contingency line items with justification fields. Format: category table with cost driver and justification columns."
7. "Act as a treasury analyst. Build a rolling 13-week cash flow forecast for [COMPANY] using actuals for weeks 1-4 and forecast for weeks 5-13. Include weekly payroll, A/R collections, and vendor payouts. Format: week-by-week table with running balance and minimum-cash alert line."
8. "You are a capital planning analyst. Draft a capital expenditure (CapEx) request for [PROJECT: new office, software platform, equipment]. Include ROI calculation, NPV, payback period, and alternative analysis. Format: one-page request with decision box for the approver."
9. "Act as a scenario planning expert. Create a scenario planning model for [BUSINESS] with base, bear, and bull cases. Vary revenue growth by -20%, 0%, +20% and show impact on EBITDA and cash. Format: 3-column comparison table plus the trigger points that would move us between cases."
10. "You are a CFO presentation writer. Write a board presentation summarizing Q[QUARTER] financial performance. Include highlights, lowlights, key metrics, and forward-looking guidance for Q[NEXT QUARTER]. Format: 8 slides, one message per slide, appendix with detailed tables."

## Lending & Credit

11. "You are a commercial credit analyst. Write a credit memo for a $[AMOUNT] [LOAN TYPE: term, line of credit, SBA] to [BORROWER] for [PURPOSE]. Include borrower background, financial analysis, collateral description, debt service coverage ratio, and recommendation. Format: standard credit memo with approval recommendation box."
12. "Act as a lending compliance officer. Create a loan denial letter template that is compliant with ECOA. Include specific reasons for denial, adverse action notice requirements, and next steps for the applicant. Format: regulatory-compliant letter with required disclosures listed."
13. "You are a debt capital advisor. Draft a term sheet for a $[AMOUNT] venture debt facility with [INTEREST RATE]% interest, [TERM] months, warrants, and financial covenants. Format: term sheet with deal table, then covenant definitions section."
14. "Act as a debt schedule analyst. Build a debt schedule for [COMPANY] with existing loans, proposed new facility, amortization schedule, and covenant compliance tracking. Format: loan-by-loan table, combined amortization schedule, and covenant headroom dashboard."
15. "You are a mortgage lending specialist. Write a mortgage pre-approval letter for [CLIENT NAME] for $[AMOUNT] at [INTEREST RATE]%. Include conditions, expiration, and next steps for home shopping. Format: professional letter plus the internal conditions checklist."

## Risk & Compliance

16. "You are an enterprise risk manager. Create a financial risk register for [COMPANY/INDUSTRY]. Include market risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, and regulatory risk. Rate likelihood and impact, and assign mitigation owners. Format: sortable risk table with heat-map summary."
17. "Act as a compliance officer. Draft an anti-money laundering (AML) policy for a [BANK/FINTECH/BROKERAGE]. Include customer due diligence (CDD), suspicious activity reporting (SAR), transaction monitoring, and record retention. Format: policy document with numbered sections and responsibility matrix."
18. "You are a financial controls expert. Write a financial controls checklist for month-end close. Include segregation of duties, reconciliation requirements, approval thresholds, and GAAP compliance checks. Format: checkbox checklist with owner and evidence columns."
19. "Act as a procurement risk specialist. Create a vendor onboarding financial due diligence questionnaire. Cover financial stability, insurance, bonding, references, and conflict-of-interest disclosures. Format: scored questionnaire with pass/fail thresholds."
20. "You are a fraud response planner. Draft a financial fraud response plan for [COMPANY]. Include detection signals, escalation matrix, forensic accounting engagement, regulatory notification timelines, and communication protocols. Format: response playbook with first-24-hours checklist."

## Personal Finance & Wealth

21. "You are a fiduciary financial planner (educational framing, not individualized advice). Explain to a client earning $[INCOME]/year with $[SAVINGS] saved and goals [GOALS: retirement at X, house in Y years] how to prioritize: emergency fund size, debt payoff order (avalanche vs. snowball math), retirement contributions, and taxable investing. Format: priority ladder with dollar allocations and the reasoning for each step."
22. "Act as a retirement projection specialist. Model retirement readiness for someone [AGE] years old with $[CURRENT BALANCE] saved, contributing $[MONTHLY]/month, planning to retire at [AGE]: the 4% rule check, the impact of [EXPECTED RETURN]% vs. conservative returns, and the 3 levers that most change the outcome. Format: projection table with scenarios."
23. "You are a debt strategist. My situation: $[DEBT BREAKDOWN: credit card, student loan, auto, mortgage] at [RATES]. Build the payoff plan: which order and why (math vs. psychology tradeoff), monthly payment schedule, total interest saved vs. minimum payments, and the balance-transfer/consolidation analysis with honest risks. Format: amortization summary table."
24. "Act as a tax-aware investing explainer. Explain to a [TAX BRACKET] investor: the account priority order (401k match → HSA → IRA → taxable), the difference between tax-deferred, Roth, and taxable accounts, tax-loss harvesting in plain English, and the 3 mistakes that trigger avoidable tax bills. Format: decision tree plus a one-page summary."
25. "You are a personal finance educator. Create the 90-day money reset program for someone [SITUATION: e.g., living paycheck to paycheck, first job, post-divorce]: week-by-week (track spending, build the $1,000 starter fund, automate, negotiate bills, start investing), each week's specific actions, and the metric that proves progress. Format: weekly checklist."

## Fintech, Payments & Financial Products

Format: pricing table with margin math per transaction."
27. "Act as a fintech compliance strategist. My product [PRODUCT: budgeting app, lending platform, investing app] handles [DATA TYPE]. Map the regulatory surface: which rules apply (Reg E, Reg Z, KYC, PCI, GLBA, state licensing), the compliance MVP vs. later-stage requirements, and the approximate cost of each. Format: regulation table with applicability verdicts."
28. "You are a financial product designer. Design a [PRODUCT: savings app, credit builder, robo-advisor feature] for [AUDIENCE: gig workers, teens, immigrants, retirees]: the behavioral principle behind each feature (default, friction, reward), the engagement loop, and how the business model aligns with user outcomes. Format: feature spec with user story."
29. "Act as a banking partnership advisor. My fintech needs a [BANK PARTNER TYPE: sponsor bank, BaaS] for [FUNCTION: deposits, lending, cards]. Explain the landscape, the questions to ask a prospective partner (compliance burden split, fees, SLAs), the red flags, and the 6-month onboarding timeline. Format: evaluation scorecard."
30. "You are an open banking / API strategist. Plan the API monetization for [FINANCIAL INSTITUTION]: which endpoints to open (accounts, payments, data), the developer tiering (free, paid, enterprise), rate limits and pricing, and the security requirements (OAuth flows, consent management). Format: tier table plus API roadmap."

Format: audit binder outline."

Format: process doc with checklist."
