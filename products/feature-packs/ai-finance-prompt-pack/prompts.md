# AI Finance Prompt Pack - 20 Prompts

## Financial Analysis & Reporting
1. "Analyze the financial statements for [COMPANY NAME] for the period [DATE RANGE]. Calculate and interpret revenue growth, gross margin, operating margin, ROE, and current ratio. Identify 3 strengths and 3 risks."
2. "Write a variance analysis report comparing actual results vs. budget for [DEPARTMENT/PRODUCT] in [PERIOD]. Explain key drivers of favorable and unfavorable variances."
3. "Create a 12-month cash flow forecast for [BUSINESS MODEL: SaaS, e-commerce, services]. Include operating, investing, and financing activities. Highlight potential cash shortfalls and mitigation strategies."
4. "Build a DCF valuation model for [COMPANY] with assumptions for revenue growth, EBITDA margin, WACC, and terminal growth rate. Show sensitivity analysis for ±10% changes in WACC and growth."
5. "Write an investment memorandum for [PROJECT/ACQUISITION] covering thesis, market size, financial projections, risks, and recommended allocation. Length: 3 pages."

## Budgeting & Forecasting
6. "Create a zero-based budget template for a [DEPARTMENT: marketing, engineering, operations] team. Include headcount, software, campaigns, and contingency line items with justification fields."
7. "Build a rolling 13-week cash flow forecast for [COMPANY] using actuals for weeks 1-4 and forecast for weeks 5-13. Include weekly payroll, A/R collections, and vendor payouts."
8. "Draft a capital expenditure (CapEx) request for [PROJECT: new office, software platform, equipment]. Include ROI calculation, NPV, payback period, and alternative analysis."
9. "Create a scenario planning model for [BUSINESS] with base, bear, and bull cases. Vary revenue growth by -20%, 0%, +20% and show impact on EBITDA and cash."
10. "Write a board presentation summarizing Q[QUARTER] financial performance. Include highlights, lowlights, key metrics, and forward-looking guidance for Q[NEXT QUARTER]."

## Lending & Credit
11. "Write a credit memo for a $[AMOUNT] [LOAN TYPE: term, line of credit, SBA] to [BORROWER] for [PURPOSE]. Include borrower background, financial analysis, collateral description, debt service coverage ratio, and recommendation."
12. "Create a loan denial letter template that is compliant with ECOA. Include specific reasons for denial, adverse action notice requirements, and next steps for the applicant."
13. "Draft a term sheet for a $[AMOUNT] venture debt facility with [INTEREST RATE]% interest, [TERM] months, warrants, and financial covenants."
14. "Build a debt schedule for [COMPANY] with existing loans, proposed new facility, amortization schedule, and covenant compliance tracking."
15. "Write a mortgage pre-approval letter for [CLIENT NAME] for $[AMOUNT] at [INTEREST RATE]%. Include conditions, expiration, and next steps for home shopping."

## Risk & Compliance
16. "Create a financial risk register for [COMPANY/INDUSTRY]. Include market risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, and regulatory risk. Rate likelihood and impact, and assign mitigation owners."
17. "Draft an anti-money laundering (AML) policy for a [BANK/FINTECH/BROKERAGE]. Include customer due diligence (CDD), suspicious activity reporting (SAR), transaction monitoring, and record retention."
18. "Write a financial controls checklist for month-end close. Include segregation of duties, reconciliation requirements, approval thresholds, and GAAP compliance checks."
19. "Create a vendor onboarding financial due diligence questionnaire. Cover financial stability, insurance, bonding, references, and conflict-of-interest disclosures."
20. "Draft a financial fraud response plan for [COMPANY]. Include detection signals, escalation matrix, forensic accounting engagement, regulatory notification timelines, and communication protocols."
