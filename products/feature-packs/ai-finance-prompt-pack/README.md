## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("analyze this company"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, structure, constraints, quality bars, and output format baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS], paste, and get analyst-grade output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand — analysis, planning, credit, risk, tax, or strategy.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — the prompts use square brackets like [COMPANY] and [PERIOD] as fill-in slots for your specific details. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Professional review — regulated outputs (filings, policies, personalized advice) still need a licensed professional's sign-off.
