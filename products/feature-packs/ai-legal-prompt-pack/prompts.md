# AI Legal Prompt Pack - 40 Expert Prompts

> How to use: Copy any prompt below, replace the [BRACKETED] variables with your details, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Each prompt includes a role, context, task, and output format for professional-grade results. These prompts support legal work but do not replace advice from a licensed attorney.

## Contracts & Agreements

1. "You are a commercial contracts attorney. Draft a freelance services agreement for [SERVICE TYPE] between [CLIENT NAME] and [CONTRACTOR NAME]. Include payment terms, deliverables, IP ownership, and termination clause. Format: numbered contract sections with defined terms."
2. "Act as a contract review specialist. Review this contract for red flags: [PASTE CONTRACT TEXT]. Flag any unfair terms, missing clauses, or liability risks for [PARTY NAME]. Format: issue list with clause reference, risk level (high/medium/low), and recommended revision language."
3. "You are a transactions lawyer. Write a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) for [SCENARIO: e.g., employee onboarding, vendor partnership, investor discussion]. Include definition of confidential information, term length, and remedies. Format: mutual or one-way (state which fits the scenario and why), standard NDA structure."
4. "Act as a privacy counsel. Create a terms of service and privacy policy for a [WEBSITE/APP TYPE] that complies with GDPR and CCPA. Include data collection, cookies, user rights, and dispute resolution. Format: plain-language policy with the required disclosures and version block."
5. "You are a lending documents attorney. Draft a promissory note for a $[AMOUNT] loan between [LENDER] and [BORROWER] with [INTEREST RATE]% interest, [TERM LENGTH] term, and default provisions. Format: note with payment schedule, acceleration clause, and signature block."
6. "You are an SaaS contract specialist. Draft a master services agreement (MSA) for [SERVICE] between [PROVIDER] and [CUSTOMER] with SLAs at [UPTIME]%, liability cap of [MULTIPLE]x fees, data processing terms, and IP provisions. Format: MSA with schedules (SLA, DPA, fee table)."
7. "Act as an employment law advisor. Draft an offer letter and separation agreement template for a [STATE] employer: at-will language where applicable, non-compete and non-solicit enforceability notes for [STATE], COBRA/ADEA/OWBPA disclosure requirements for separations, and the release language checklist. Format: letter plus annotated template."
8. "You are a commercial leasing attorney. Draft a commercial sublease agreement for [PREMISES] in [CITY]: the master lease acknowledgment, rent and pass-through structure, term, assignment restrictions, and landlord consent conditions. Format: sublease with required master lease provisions incorporated by reference."

## Legal Research & Analysis

9. "Act as a legal researcher. Summarize the key holdings and legal reasoning of [CASE NAME OR CITATION]. Explain how it applies to [FACT PATTERN] in [JURISDICTION]. Format: IRAC summary (issue, rule, application, conclusion) with the key quotes and a plain-English takeaway."
10. "You are a litigation strategist. Identify the elements of [CAUSE OF ACTION: e.g., breach of contract, negligence, fraud] under [STATE] law and list the evidence needed to prove each element. Format: element table with evidence column, admissibility notes, and the weakest element flagged."
11. "Act as a regulatory comparison analyst. Compare and contrast [STATUTE OR REGULATION A] vs. [STATUTE OR REGULATION B] as they apply to [INDUSTRY OR SCENARIO]. Include compliance checklist. Format: side-by-side comparison table, then a dual-compliance checklist for an entity subject to both."
12. "You are a legal writing expert. Draft a legal memorandum analyzing whether [FACT PATTERN] constitutes [LEGAL ISSUE] under current [STATE/FEDERAL] law. Include rule statement, analysis, and conclusion. Format: standard memo (question presented, brief answer, facts, discussion, conclusion) with counterarguments addressed."
13. "Act as a discovery strategist. Create a deposition outline for [WITNESS ROLE] in a [CASE TYPE] lawsuit. Cover 15 questions covering direct examination, impeachment, and key facts. Format: organized outline by topic with exhibit references and impeachment setup questions."
14. "You are an appeals specialist. Draft the issues-presented section for an appeal of [TRIAL OUTCOME] in [CASE TYPE]: the standard of review for each issue (de novo, abuse of discretion, clear error) with why it matters, the preserved-vs-unpreserved-error analysis, and the strongest 3 issues ranked by likelihood of success. Format: issue statements in the 'whether' format with roadmap."
15. "Act as a 50-state survey researcher. Survey how [STATUTE TYPE: e.g., non-compete enforcement, data breach notification, meal break rules] is treated across [LIST STATES OR 'all 50 states']: the majority rule, the minority approaches, the strictest and most lenient states, and where my [STATE] falls. Format: state-by-state summary table with the practical takeaway for a [CLIENT TYPE]."
16. "You are a legal due diligence associate. Build the diligence checklist for acquiring a [TARGET TYPE: SaaS company, restaurant chain, medical practice]: corporate records, IP assignments, customer contracts with change-of-control clauses, employment matters, litigation search, and regulatory licenses. Format: checklist with the 'why it matters' note per item."

Draft the difficult-update email to [CLIENT] about [BAD NEWS: weak case development, settlement recommendation, fee overrun]: honest assessment, options with pros/cons, clear recommendation, and the decision the client must make.
