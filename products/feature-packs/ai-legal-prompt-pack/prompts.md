# AI Legal Prompt Pack - 20 Prompts

## Contracts & Agreements
1. "Draft a freelance services agreement for [SERVICE TYPE] between [CLIENT NAME] and [CONTRACTOR NAME]. Include payment terms, deliverables, IP ownership, and termination clause."
2. "Review this contract for red flags: [PASTE CONTRACT TEXT]. Flag any unfair terms, missing clauses, or liability risks for [PARTY NAME]."
3. "Write a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) for [SCENARIO: e.g., employee onboarding, vendor partnership, investor discussion]. Include definition of confidential information, term length, and remedies."
4. "Create a terms of service and privacy policy for a [WEBSITE/APP TYPE] that complies with GDPR and CCPA. Include data collection, cookies, user rights, and dispute resolution."
5. "Draft a promissory note for a $[AMOUNT] loan between [LENDER] and [BORROWER] with [INTEREST RATE]% interest, [TERM LENGTH] term, and default provisions."

## Legal Research & Analysis
6. "Summarize the key holdings and legal reasoning of [CASE NAME OR CITATION]. Explain how it applies to [FACT PATTERN] in [JURISDICTION]."
7. "Identify the elements of [CAUSE OF ACTION: e.g., breach of contract, negligence, fraud] under [STATE] law and list the evidence needed to prove each element."
8. "Compare and contrast [STATUTE OR REGULATION A] vs. [STATUTE OR REGULATION B] as they apply to [INDUSTRY OR SCENARIO]. Include compliance checklist."
9. "Draft a legal memorandum analyzing whether [FACT PATTERN] constitutes [LEGAL ISSUE] under current [STATE/FEDERAL] law. Include rule statement, analysis, and conclusion."
10. "Create a deposition outline for [WITNESS ROLE] in a [CASE TYPE] lawsuit. Cover 15 questions covering direct examination, impeachment, and key facts."

## Client Intake & Documents
11. "Draft a client intake form for a [PRACTICE AREA: e.g., personal injury, family law, corporate] law firm. Include conflict check questions, case facts, and billing preferences."
12. "Write a demand letter to [PARTY NAME] for [CLAIM: e.g., unpaid invoice, breach of contract, property damage]. State facts, legal basis, damages, and deadline for response."
13. "Create a retainer agreement template for hourly legal services. Include scope, hourly rates, fee estimates, billing cycle, and cost advancements."
14. "Draft a cease-and-desist letter for [VIOLATION: e.g., trademark infringement, copyright violation, defamation]. Include factual background, legal basis, and remedies demanded."
15. "Write a power of attorney document for [GRANTOR] appointing [AGENT] with authority over [SCOPE: financial, healthcare, real estate] in [STATE]."

## Compliance & Risk
16. "Conduct a compliance audit checklist for [INDUSTRY: e.g., healthcare, fintech, SaaS] under [REGULATION: HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR]. Include documentation requirements and risk ratings."
17. "Draft an internal whistleblower policy for a [COMPANY SIZE] [INDUSTRY] company. Include reporting channels, anti-retaliation protections, and investigation procedures."
18. "Create a data processing agreement (DPA) for a [CONTROLLER/PROCESSOR] relationship under GDPR. Include Article 28 requirements, subprocessor rules, and security measures."
19. "Write a regulatory response plan for a [AGENCY: SEC, FTC, CFPB] inquiry or audit. Include document preservation, internal communication protocols, and attorney engagement."
20. "Draft an arbitration clause for [CONTRACT TYPE] with rules for [ARBITRATION BODY: AAA, JAMS]. Include selection of arbitrator, governing law, and appeal rights."
