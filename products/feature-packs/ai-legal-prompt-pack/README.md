## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("draft an NDA"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, structure, constraints, quality bars, and output format baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS], paste, and get professional drafting output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand — transactional, research, compliance, or litigation.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — the prompts use square brackets like [STATE] and [PASTE CONTRACT] as fill-in slots for your specific details. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Attorney review — these prompts produce drafting acceleration, not legal advice. A licensed attorney must review jurisdiction-specific output before use.
