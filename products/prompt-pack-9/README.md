## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("validate my startup idea"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, context, structure, constraints, quality bars, and output format baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS], paste, and get operator-grade output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the stage you're in — validation, build, customers, growth, funding, team, or resilience.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — the prompts use square brackets like [IDEA] and [AUDIENCE] as fill-in slots for your specific details. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Adapt the encoded frameworks to your own judgment and market.
