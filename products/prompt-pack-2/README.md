## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("write a function that does X"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, explicit constraints, output structure, quality bars, and "after the code" deliverables like failure modes and verification steps baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS], paste your real code and errors, and get senior-engineer-grade output.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — paste real code, real stack traces, real scale numbers. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Review before shipping — the prompts encode professional structures, but you own what goes to production.
