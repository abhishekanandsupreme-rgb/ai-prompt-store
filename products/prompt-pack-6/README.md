## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("make me more productive"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, the specific framework, realistic constraints ("must survive your worst week, not your best one"), scripts written word-for-word, and honest-tradeoff notes baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS] with your real schedule and lists, paste, and get coach-level systems you can actually keep.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — real task lists, real hours, real energy patterns. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Start smaller than you want to — the prompts encode that principle deliberately.
