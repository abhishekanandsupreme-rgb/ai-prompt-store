## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("analyze my business"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, explicit structure, constraints against vagueness, quality bars, and honest-note requirements baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS] with your real numbers and context, paste, and get consultant-grade output you can act on.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — real revenue numbers, real customer quotes, real constraints. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Own the decisions — verify anything that touches money, law, or hiring before acting on it.
