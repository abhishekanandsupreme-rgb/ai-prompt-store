## Why these prompts are different

Most prompt packs give you one-line commands ("make my character deeper"). These are **full expert briefs** — role framing, the specific craft framework, diagnostic questions, rewrite requirements with annotation, and honest-tradeoff notes baked in. You fill the [BRACKETS] with your real draft or premise, paste, and get editor-level developmental notes.

## How to use

1. Pick the section for the job at hand.
2. Fill every [BRACKET] — paste real drafts, real word counts, real genre. Specificity in, quality out.
3. Iterate on the 80%-right output instead of regenerating.
4. Keep your voice — the prompts encode craft structures; the sentences are yours.
