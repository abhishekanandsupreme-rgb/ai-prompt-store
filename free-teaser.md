## Content & Marketing

### 1. The 10-Minute YouTube Script
*From the AI Content Creator Pack — never script a video from a blank page again.*

```
Write a 10-minute YouTube script about [TOPIC] for an audience of [AUDIENCE].

Structure it as:
1. A 15-second hook that states the payoff ("By the end of this video, you'll...")
2. A 30-second intro that previews the 3 main points
3. Three main points — each with a real example, a common mistake to avoid, and one actionable takeaway
4. A CTA asking viewers to [SUBSCRIBE / COMMENT / CLICK THE LINK]

Write it conversationally at an 8th-grade reading level, and add [B-ROLL / ON-SCREEN TEXT] suggestions in brackets throughout.
```

### 2. The 5-Email Welcome Sequence
*From the AI Content Creator Pack — the exact sequence structure every info-product business runs.*

```
Write a 5-email welcome sequence for a [BUSINESS TYPE] that sells [PRODUCT/SERVICE] to [AUDIENCE].

Email 1: Deliver the lead magnet and set expectations
Email 2: Founder story + the problem you solve
Email 3: Customer results / social proof
Email 4: Handle the #1 objection ("[COMMON OBJECTION]")
Email 5: Pitch [PRODUCT] with a deadline of [DEADLINE]

For each email give me: a subject line under 45 characters, preview text, and a body under 200 words with exactly one CTA.
```

### 3. The Instant Customer Persona
*From the AI Business Builder Pack — a persona deep enough to paste straight into an ad campaign.*

```
Act as a senior market researcher. Create a detailed customer persona for a [NICHE] buyer who is likely to buy [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Include: demographics, job title, 5 daily frustrations, 3 goals, 3 fears, where they hang out online, what they've already tried and why it failed, their top 3 objections to buying, and the exact words and phrases they use to describe the problem.

Finish with a 3-sentence summary I can paste into an ad campaign brief.
```

### 4. Subject Lines Built for Open Rates
*From the AI Marketing Master Pack — 10 subject lines with the psychology labeled.*

```
Write 10 email subject lines for an email about [OFFER/ANNOUNCEMENT] aimed at [AUDIENCE].

Mix these proven angles: curiosity gap, specific number, fear of missing out, direct benefit, and personal ("you"/"your").

Rules: under 45 characters, no clickbait the body can't pay off, no spam-trigger words.
For each one, add preview text (under 80 characters) and label which angle it uses.
Mark your top 3 picks for an A/B test.
```

### 5. The Sales Page Skeleton
*From the AI Marketing Master Pack — full long-form sales copy structure in one prompt.*

```
Write sales page copy for [PRODUCT] that sells to [AUDIENCE] at [PRICE].

Include: an outcome-driven headline, a subheadline saying who it's for, a problem section that agitates the pain, the solution, 5 benefit bullets (outcome + mechanism), 3 testimonial placeholders, an offer stack, the price anchored against [COMPARE-AT PRICE], a [GUARANTEE], a 5-question FAQ that answers real objections, and CTAs at the top, middle, and end.

Tone: [TONE]. Use "you" language. Every section must end with a reason to keep reading.
```

## Code & Technical Work

### 6. The Code Debugger That Explains Itself
*From the AI Coding Assistant Pack — finds the bug AND teaches you why it broke.*

```
Debug this code:

[PASTE CODE]

Do the following:
1. Identify every bug and explain each in plain English (what's wrong and why it breaks)
2. Rank them by severity
3. Give me the corrected code with the changed lines commented
4. Name one edge case that would still make the fixed version fail

Language: [LANGUAGE]. Expected behavior: [WHAT IT SHOULD DO]. Actual behavior: [WHAT HAPPENS].
```

### 7. Unit Tests You Can Actually Ship
*From the AI Coding Assistant Pack — tests named like sentences, edge cases included.*

```
Write unit tests for this function: [PASTE FUNCTION]

Requirements:
- Test framework: [JEST / PYTEST / OTHER]
- Cover the happy path, edge cases (empty input, zeros, nulls, unicode), and failure cases
- Use descriptive test names that read like sentences ("returns an empty array when input is empty")
- Mock [EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES] — do not hit real services

Output only the test file, ready to run.
```

## Writing & Storytelling

### 8. The Character Bible Entry
*From the AI Writing Assistant Pack — a backstory built for writing, not a Wikipedia dump.*

```
Create a character backstory for [CHARACTER CONCEPT] in a [GENRE] story.

Include: a defining childhood event, a wound that still drives them, what they want vs. what they actually need, one contradiction that makes them feel human, their voice (3 sample lines of dialogue), and how they react under pressure (fight, freeze, or charm?).

Keep it under 400 words — usable as a writing-bible entry.
```

## Productivity & Planning

### 9. The Time-Blocking Schedule
*From the AI Productivity Pack — a realistic day built around deep work, not vibes.*

```
Act as a productivity coach. Build me a time-blocking schedule for a [ROLE] whose fixed commitments are [MEETINGS/OBLIGATIONS] and whose top 3 priorities this week are [P1], [P2], and [P3].

Rules: deep-work blocks of 90 minutes maximum, a break after every 90 minutes, all shallow work (email, admin) batched into 2 blocks, and [1 HOUR] protected for weekly planning on Monday.

Output a table: time, block name, block type (deep / shallow / rest), and which priority it serves.
```

## Social Media

### 10. The 30-Day Content Calendar
*From the AI Social Media Manager Pack — a month of content, pillars pre-balanced.*

```
Create a 30-day content calendar for [NICHE/BRAND] on [PLATFORM].

For each day include: format (post / reel / thread / carousel), the hook (first line), a body outline, a CTA, and the best posting time for [AUDIENCE TIMEZONE].

Rotate these pillars: educational (40%), entertaining (25%), behind-the-scenes (20%), promotional (15%).

Mark the 5 posts most likely to go viral and explain why in one line each.
```

### 11. The Thread That Gets Read to the End
*From the AI Social Media Manager Pack — hook, payoff, and A/B alternatives included.*

```
Write a Twitter/X thread for [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC].

Rules: a hook tweet under 280 characters that promises a specific payoff, 7-10 tweets, one idea per tweet, short lines with line breaks, a concrete example or number in at least half the tweets, and a final tweet with a soft CTA to [FOLLOW / DM / CLICK].

Also give me 3 alternative hooks so I can A/B test the opener.
```

## SEO

### 12. The 90-Day Keyword Strategy
*From the AI SEO Expert Pack — keyword research that assumes you're starting from zero.*

```
Act as an SEO strategist. Build a keyword research strategy for a [WEBSITE TYPE] in the [NICHE] space, targeting [AUDIENCE].

Give me: 10 seed keywords, 30 long-tail variations grouped by intent (informational / commercial / transactional), a priority score for each (volume x achievability for a new site), and the 5 keywords to target in the first 90 days with your reasoning.

Present it as a table. Assume I only have [FREE TOOLS / TOOL ACCESS].
```

### 13. Cold Outreach That Gets Replies
*From the AI SEO Expert Pack — link-building emails that don't smell like templates.*

```
Write a link-building outreach email to a [SITE TYPE] editor about my article on [TOPIC].

Rules: under 120 words, no "I love your blog" flattery, lead with what's in it for them (their reader, their traffic, or a gap in their content), one clear ask (link / mention / guest swap), and a subject line that doesn't look like a mass email.

Give me 3 subject line options and a 2-sentence follow-up for if there's no reply after 5 days.
```

## Startups & Teaching

### 14. Kill Your Idea in 2 Weeks (Validation Framework)
*From the AI Entrepreneur Pack — find out the idea is bad BEFORE you build it.*

```
Act as a startup advisor. Create a validation framework for my idea: [DESCRIBE IDEA], targeting [AUDIENCE].

Structure it as a 2-week plan with daily actions: how to find 10 target customers to talk to, exact customer interview questions (problem-first, never "would you buy this?"), a fake-door landing page test, the specific signal that means "go", and the 3 cheapest ways to kill the idea fast.

End with a go/no-go checklist.
```

### 15. The Quiz Generator
*From the AI Education Pack — questions, distractors, and the answer key in one shot.*

```
Create a [NUMBER]-question quiz on [TOPIC] for [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED] students.

Mix question types: multiple choice (4 options), true/false, and short answer.

For each question include: the correct answer, a one-line explanation of why, and the common wrong answer students pick and why.

End with an answer key and 3 bonus challenge questions for students who finish early.
```
